Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

A payment webhook that marks an invoice paid, a bank feed that doesn't match, a USD invoice collected at a new rate, a month that must stay closed: this is where integrations with an ERP fail quietly. The questions you will be asked in production are "did the money really arrive?", "why is this account off by 400?" and "who changed that entry?".

This lesson answers them from a real Odoo 20 Community lab, and it corrects a belief most developers hold: that a posted entry can't be changed. By default, it can. The lock and immutability behaviours were tested as superuser and as normal accounting users.

بالعامية

webhook دفع بيعلّم فاتورة paid، bank feed مش مطابق، فاتورة بالدولار اتحصّلت بسعر جديد، شهر لازم يفضل مقفول: هنا بالظبط الـ integrations مع الـ ERP بتقع من غير صوت. الأسئلة اللي هتتسألها في production: "الفلوس وصلت فعلًا؟"، "الحساب ده ليه ناقص 400؟"، "مين عدّل القيد ده؟".

الدرس ده بيجاوبهم من lab حقيقي على Odoo 20 Community، وبيصحح اعتقاد عند معظم المبرمجين: إن القيد الـ posted مبيتغيرش. في الإعدادات الافتراضية، بيتغير. وسلوك الـ lock dates والثبات اتجرب بـ superuser وبمستخدمين محاسبة عاديين.

Part 1 — Payments and reconciliation 🔴 Advancedالجزء 1 — الدفعات والـ reconciliation

English

The wizard account.payment.register creates an account.payment, which owns an account.move, and then reconciles it with the invoice. Reconciliation means matching debit lines against credit lines on one account. Nothing about the amounts in the ledger changes: Odoo only records which line settles which.

Each match is a row in account.partial.reconcile with debit_move_id, credit_move_id and amount. amount_residual on a line is its balance minus its matches, and payment_state on the invoice follows from that: not_paid, partial, paid. When a connected group of lines reaches zero, Odoo adds one account.full.reconcile. matching_number shows it: P9 while partial, a plain number once full.

Because it's only links, it's reversible: remove_move_reconcile deletes the partials and the full, and the payment stays posted. A payment made before the invoice stays as an open credit on the customer until you assign it. A small shortfall can be closed with a write-off line.

Don't confuse this with lesson 06. There, reconciliation compared your ledger with an external provider report. Here it links two lines inside one ledger. Accountants use the same word for both.

   invoice receivable line              payment receivable lines
   debit 1000  (id 450)                 credit 400  (id 452)
          |  \                          credit 600  (id 454)
          |   \
          |    +--- partial: 450 <-> 452, amount 400 ---+
          |                                             |
          +-------- partial: 450 <-> 454, amount 600 ---+
                                                        |
                       residual 0  =>  full reconcile (one row, 3 lines)
سطر الـ receivable في الفاتورة مدين 1000. الدفعتين كل واحدة سطر دائن. كل ربط بينهم صف partial بمبلغه (400 و600). لما الـ residual يوصل صفر، بيتعمل صف full reconcile واحد بيجمع التلات سطور.

بالعامية

الـ wizard account.payment.register بيعمل account.payment، وده ليه account.move، وبعدين بيربطه بالفاتورة. الـ reconciliation معناها ربط سطور مدينة بسطور دائنة على نفس الحساب. مفيش أي مبلغ في الدفاتر بيتغير: Odoo بيسجل بس مين بيسدد مين.

كل ربط صف في account.partial.reconcile فيه debit_move_id وcredit_move_id وamount. وamount_residual على السطر هو قيمته ناقص ربطاته، وpayment_state على الفاتورة بيتبني عليه: not_paid أو partial أو paid. ولما مجموعة سطور مترابطة توصل صفر، Odoo بيضيف صف account.full.reconcile واحد. وmatching_number بيوريك ده: P9 وهو partial، ورقم عادي لما يبقى full.

وبما إنها مجرد روابط، فهي بتترجع: remove_move_reconcile بيمسح الـ partials والـ full والدفعة بتفضل posted. والدفعة اللي جت قبل الفاتورة بتقعد credit مفتوح على العميل لحد ما تربطها. والفرق الصغير ممكن يتقفل بسطر write-off.

متخلطش ده بـ درس 06. هناك الـ reconciliation كانت مقارنة الـ ledger بتقرير provider خارجي. هنا ربط سطرين جوه نفس الـ ledger. والمحاسبين بيستخدموا نفس الكلمة للاتنين.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ الـ webhook بتاع Stripe أو Paymob وصل: الـ handler بتاعك يعمل payment ويربطه بالفاتورة، ولو الدفع جزئي تسيب partial زي ما هي. متكتبش payment_state بإيدك أبدًا، ده محسوب. ولو العميل دفع deposit قبل الأوردر، سجله payment لوحده وابقى اربطه لما الفاتورة تطلع. الغلطة الشائعة: تعمل refund بمسح الـ payment بدل ما تفك الربط وتعكس القيد.
Interview question "How is an invoice's paid status derived, and what happens if you undo a payment?" → It's derived, never stored by hand: partial-reconcile rows reduce amount_residual, and payment_state follows. Undoing deletes the links; the payment's journal entry stays, leaving an open credit on the customer until it's matched or reversed.
"حالة paid في الفاتورة بتيجي منين، وإيه اللي بيحصل لو رجعت في دفعة؟" ← محسوبة ومش بتتكتب بإيد: صفوف الـ partial reconcile بتقلل amount_residual وpayment_state بيتبعه. التراجع بيمسح الروابط بس، وقيد الدفعة بيفضل، فبيبقى فيه credit مفتوح على العميل لحد ما يتربط أو يتعكس.

Part 2 — Outstanding accounts 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 2 — الـ outstanding accounts

English

A registered payment does not debit the bank account. In the run it debits 150800 Outstanding Receipts and credits receivable 150100. The bank account 160100 moves only when a bank statement line confirms the money.

In programmer terms, there are two asynchronous facts awaiting correlation: "we recorded that the customer paid" and "the bank says money arrived". They arrive at different times, from different sources, sometimes in different amounts. The outstanding account is the holding area between them, and its balance is exactly the money in flight. Think of a payment_intent.succeeded event and a later settlement report joined on a key.

Two consequences. First, in Community the invoice reads paid as soon as the payment is reconciled, before any bank confirmation. The docstring of _get_invoice_in_payment_state says the accountant module overrides it to enable in_payment; that module isn't in this source. Trust payment.is_matched for "the bank confirmed". Second, the source forces an outstanding account in Community even when the payment method has none, so that a journal entry always exists.

  fact 1: "customer paid"                fact 2: "bank says arrived"
  account.payment                        account.bank.statement.line
         |                                          |
         v                                          v
  D 150800 outstanding                   D 160100 bank
  C 150100 receivable                    C 160103 suspense
         |                                          |
         |          correlate (reference, amount)   |
         +------------------> match <---------------+
                                |
                   C 150800 replaces C 160103
              outstanding back to 0, is_matched = True
شمال: الحقيقة الأولى "العميل دفع" بتطلّع قيد مدين 150800 ودائن 150100. يمين: الحقيقة التانية "البنك استلم" بتطلّع مدين 160100 ودائن 160103 (suspense). لما نربطهم بالمرجع والمبلغ، سطر الـ suspense بيتحول لدائن 150800، فالـ outstanding يرجع صفر وis_matched يبقى True.

بالعامية

الدفعة لما تتسجل مش بتزوّد حساب البنك. في الـ run مدين 150800 Outstanding Receipts ودائن receivable 150100. حساب البنك 160100 مبيتحركش غير لما سطر كشف حساب يأكد الفلوس.

بلغة المبرمجين، عندك حقيقتين async مستنيين يترابطوا: "سجلنا إن العميل دفع" و"البنك بيقول الفلوس وصلت". بييجوا في أوقات مختلفة ومن مصادر مختلفة وأحيانًا بمبالغ مختلفة. الـ outstanding account هو منطقة الانتظار بينهم، ورصيده هو بالظبط الفلوس اللي في الطريق. زي event payment_intent.succeeded وبعدها settlement report بيترابطوا بمفتاح.

فيه نتيجتين. الأولى: في الـ Community الفاتورة بتبقى paid أول ما الدفعة تتربط، قبل أي تأكيد من البنك. الـ docstring بتاع _get_invoice_in_payment_state بيقول إن موديول الـ accountant هو اللي بيفعّل in_payment، والموديول ده مش في الـ source ده. اعتمد على payment.is_matched لو عايز "البنك أكد". التانية: الكود بيجبر outstanding account في الـ Community حتى لو طريقة الدفع مفيهاش، عشان القيد يتعمل دايمًا.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ لو الـ Node service بتاعك بيشحن الأوردر لما الفاتورة تبقى paid، فأنت بتشحن على تسجيل مش على فلوس: تحويل بنكي العميل قال عمله ومطلعش حقيقي هيعدّي. استخدم is_matched للحالات دي. وراقب رصيد 150800 كـ metric: لو بيكبر من غير ما ينزل يبقى فيه دفعات مش متطابقة. ودي نفس فكرة الـ reconciliation job في case الـ payment system.

Part 3 — Bank statements 🔴 Advancedالجزء 3 — كشوف البنك

English

An account.bank.statement.line inherits account.move through _inherits, so creating one posts an entry immediately: debit the bank account, credit the journal's suspense account (160103), with is_reconciled False. Suspense means "real money, unknown reason".

Community has no bank reconciliation screen. The matching widget belongs to account_accountant, which is Enterprise and absent from this source; bank.rec.widget isn't registered. The account.reconcile.model data model does exist. So in Community you match in code: reset the statement entry to draft, repoint its suspense line to the receivable with skip_account_move_synchronization, post, then reconcile(). action_undo_reconciliation restores it.

A matcher needs a policy. Auto-apply only on strong evidence: a unique invoice number in the reference and an exact amount. A same-partner, same-amount hit is a suggestion, because a customer can have two identical invoices. Everything else goes to a person, and the report of what didn't match is the real product of the job.

def apply_match(st, inv):
    # من غير الـ widget: رجّع القيد draft، وحوّل سطر الـ suspense للـ receivable، وعلّم
    suspense = st.move_id.line_ids.filtered(lambda l: l.account_id == st.journal_id.suspense_account_id)
    target = receivable(inv)
    st.move_id.with_context(skip_readonly_check=True).button_draft()
    suspense.with_context(skip_account_move_synchronization=True).write({'account_id': target.account_id.id, 'partner_id': inv.commercial_partner_id.id})
    st.move_id.action_post()
    (suspense | target).reconcile()


def auto_match(statement_lines, candidates):
    report = []
    for st in statement_lines:

        by_ref = open_invoices.filtered(lambda i: i.name in (st.payment_ref or ''))

Part 4 — Multi-currency 🔴 Advancedالجزء 4 — تعدد العملات

English

Rates live in res.currency.rate, per company and date. inverse_company_rate is the human form (54 EGP per USD); company_rate is its inverse. Each line carries amount_currency (100 USD) and balance (5000 EGP), fixed at the rate for its date.

A USD invoice for 100 at 50 posts 5000 EGP. The customer pays 100 USD when the rate is 54, so the payment posts 5400 EGP. In USD it's settled; in EGP 400 is left over. Odoo generates an exchange move in its own journal: debit receivable 400, credit income 510200. A fall in the rate produces the opposite, a loss on 430700. The link is account.partial.reconcile.exchange_move_id; account.full.reconcile has no such field.

A partial payment realises only its share: 40 USD at 54 against a book rate of 50 gives 160. That is realised gain: the cash has moved. The remaining 60 USD still sits at 3000 EGP; valued at a closing rate of 52 it would be 3120, an unrealised gain of 120. Community has no report or entry for that, since the reports engine is Enterprise, so the script only calculates it. Undoing a reconciliation doesn't delete the exchange move: Odoo reverses it.

type Open = { usdMinor: bigint; bookEgpMinor: bigint };

const SCALE = 10_000n;
const toEgp = (usdMinor: bigint, rate: bigint): bigint => (usdMinor * rate) / SCALE;

// الدفع بيقفل جزء بسعره الدفتري، والفرق عن سعر النهارده هو الـ realised
function settle(open: Open, paidUsdMinor: bigint, rate: bigint) {
  // نصيب الجزء المدفوع من القيمة الدفترية، بنسبة الدولارات
  const bookOfPaid = (open.bookEgpMinor * paidUsdMinor) / open.usdMinor;
  const realised = toEgp(paidUsdMinor, rate) - bookOfPaid;
  const left: Open = { usdMinor: open.usdMinor - paidUsdMinor, bookEgpMinor: open.bookEgpMinor - bookOfPaid };
  return { realised, left };
}

Part 5 — Analytic accounting 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 5 — الـ analytic accounting

English

Analytics answer "which project, department or tenant?" without inflating the chart of accounts. analytic_distribution is a JSON field on the journal line: {"37": 100} means 100% to analytic account 37, {"37": 60, "38": 40} splits it, and a comma key like {"37,39": 100} tags one row on two plans at once. Rows in account.analytic.line appear at posting; a draft has none.

Each plan gets its own column on the analytic line, named x_plan<id>_id. Only the project plan uses account_id. That's a real trap: searching account_id for an account on another plan finds 0 rows while the data is there. Filter through the plan's _column_name() or through analytic_line_ids on the journal line.

Analytics are a parallel view, so they are looser than the ledger. Income is positive and expense negative, so a sum is a margin. The percentages needn't total 100. Changing a distribution after posting is allowed and doesn't touch the entry.

Part 6 — Lock dates in depth 🔴 Advancedالجزء 6 — الـ lock dates بالتفصيل

English

Five fields on res.company: fiscalyear_lock_date (global), tax_lock_date, sale_lock_date, purchase_lock_date, and hard_lock_date. The first four are soft. The hard one can only move forward: removing it or moving it back raises UserError, and it allows no exceptions.

Posting into a locked period does not fail. The accounting date moves while invoice_date stays. Where it lands comes from _get_accounting_date and depends on the journal's numbering. A yearly sales journal lands at the end of the first open year, capped at today: 2026-11-11 in the lab. A monthly purchase journal lands at the end of the first open month: 2026-04-30. So it is not simply "the day after the lock".

Entries already inside the period are protected: reset to draft and changing the account are refused, though a harmless field like ref still passes. Locking past unreconciled bank lines raises RedirectWarning, and a hard lock also refuses to pass draft entries. For a controlled reopening, account.lock_exception records a user (or everyone), the lock field, a reason and an expiry; it applies only to that user and can be revoked. In the lab an accountant couldn't write the lock fields directly: res.company needs an administrator.

Part 7 — What makes a posted entry immutable 🔴 Advancedالجزء 7 — إيه اللي بيخلي القيد المنشور ثابت فعلًا

English

The common belief is "posted entries can't be changed". On a default install that is too simple. unlink, date and partner_id are refused. But ref, a line's account, and even a line's balance pass through the ORM, and button_draft is allowed: reset, edit, repost, and the invoice keeps its number with a new total. A normal accountant and a billing user got exactly the same results as the superuser. The form hides these edits; the ORM and any API on top of it don't. Default config has no hashed journals and restrictive_audit_trail False.

Real immutability is layered, and each layer does a different job:

  • Posted-state checks stop casual edits of header fields.
  • Lock dates stop changes to a closed period (Part 6).
  • Hashing: with restrict_mode_hash_table on a journal, each entry gets inalterable_hash, a SHA-256 over its fields and the previous hash. The write guard refuses reset to draft, deletion, and changes to hashed fields such as a line's account.
  • Integrity check: _check_hash_integrity recomputes the chain. It caught a raw SQL edit, and it also caught a write to balance, which the guard let through because the guard compares field names and only debit and credit are on its list. So the check isn't optional: a hash that nobody verifies proves nothing.
  • Audit trail: restrictive_audit_trail doesn't block the account change. It keeps the record: posted entries can't be deleted and the tracking log can't be removed.

The correction for all of this is the same as in lesson 05: reverse, don't edit. A reversal in a hashed journal is itself hashed. The hash chain here is the same idea as the one you built in lesson 06.

2. A tolerance rule 🟡 medium Extend the matcher's policy: a difference up to 5.00 becomes a write-off suggestion, never automatic, and larger differences stay mismatches. Use Decimal, not float, for the comparison.
وسّع سياسة الـ matcher: الفرق لحد 5.00 يبقى اقتراح write-off ومش أوتوماتيك أبدًا، والفرق الأكبر يفضل mismatch. استخدم Decimal مش float في المقارنة.
Solution · الحل 
# بنضيف tolerance: الفرق الصغير بيتقترح مع write-off ومبيتعلّمش أوتوماتيك
from decimal import Decimal

TOLERANCE = Decimal('5.00')


def classify(st_amount, residual):
    diff = Decimal(str(residual)) - Decimal(str(st_amount))
    if diff == 0:
        return 'matched'
    if abs(diff) <= TOLERANCE:
        return f'suggest_writeoff {diff}'
    return f'amount_mismatch {diff}'


for amount, residual in [(500, 500), (497.5, 500), (280, 300)]:
    print(amount, residual, classify(amount, residual))
env.cr.rollback()

497.5 500 suggest_writeoff 2.5
280 300 amount_mismatch 20

from hashlib import sha256
from json import dumps
from odoo.tools import float_repr

secure = e['account.journal'].create({'name': 'Lab Secure', 'code': 'LSEC', 'type': 'sale', 'restrict_mode_hash_table': True})
first, second = invoice(100, journal=secure), invoice(200, journal=secure)


def value(record, name):
    raw, field = record[name], record._fields[name]
    if field.type == 'many2one':
        raw = raw.id
    if field.type == 'monetary':
        return float_repr(raw, record.currency_id.decimal_places)
    return str(raw)


def recompute(move, previous):
    # الهاش اللي قبله متخزّن بالشكل $4$abc، والجزء التالت بس هو اللي بيدخل في الحساب
    previous = previous.split('$')[2] if previous.startswith('$') else previous
    values = {name: value(move, name) for name in move._get_integrity_hash_fields()}
    for line in move.line_ids:
        for name in line._get_integrity_hash_fields():
            values[f'line_{line.id}_{name}'] = value(line, name)
    record = dumps(values, sort_keys=True, ensure_ascii=True, indent=None, separators=(',', ':'))
    return '$4$' + sha256((previous + record).encode()).hexdigest()


print('first ', recompute(first, '') == first.inalterable_hash)
print('second', recompute(second, first.inalterable_hash) == second.inalterable_hash)
env.cr.rollback()
first  True
second True

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • Reconciliation links debit and credit lines on one account: account.partial.reconcile rows, then one account.full.reconcile. Amounts don't change, and it's undoable.
  • payment_state is derived from amount_residual. Never write it.
  • A payment debits outstanding (150800), not the bank. Outstanding's balance is money in flight.
  • In Community paid doesn't mean the bank confirmed: check is_matched.
  • A statement line posts at once: debit bank, credit suspense. Community has no matching widget, so match in code.
  • Auto-apply only on reference plus exact amount. Partner plus amount is a suggestion.
  • FX difference: exchange move in its own journal, linked on the partial, reversed on undo. Unrealised isn't posted in Community.
  • Analytics: JSON distribution, rows at posting, one x_plan<id>_id column per plan.
  • Posting into a locked period moves date: end of first open month or year, capped at today, depending on the journal.
  • Soft locks can be removed or excepted with account.lock_exception; the hard lock only moves forward.
  • Default Odoo lets the ORM change a posted line and reset to draft, for ordinary accounting users too.
  • Real immutability: lock dates, a hashed journal, scheduled integrity checks, the audit trail, least-privilege keys, and reversal for corrections.

  • الـ reconciliation بتربط سطور مدينة ودائنة على نفس الحساب: صفوف account.partial.reconcile وبعدين account.full.reconcile واحد. المبالغ مبتتغيرش وبتترجع.
  • payment_state محسوب من amount_residual. عمرك ما تكتبه.
  • الدفعة بتتقيد مدين outstanding (150800) مش البنك. ورصيد الـ outstanding هو الفلوس اللي في الطريق.
  • في الـ Community paid مش معناها البنك أكد: بص على is_matched.

  • فرق العملة: exchange move في journal لوحده، مربوط على الـ partial، وبيتعكس عند التراجع. والـ unrealised مبيتقيدش في الـ Community.
