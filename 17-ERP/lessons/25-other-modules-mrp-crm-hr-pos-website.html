Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

Lessons 21–24 followed goods and money through Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Accounting. Real clients also make things, chase leads, bill hours, pay staff, run tills and sell online. You don't need to master each app, but you must know which model holds the truth, when a module touches the ledger, and which one is hard for reasons that have nothing to do with code.

Everything below ran on a private Odoo 20 lab unless it says "not run here". Two of these modules teach ideas you will reuse outside ERP: payroll is the textbook case for effective-dated rules, and the POS is a production offline-first client with real conflict rules.

بالعامية

في الدروس 21–24 مشينا ورا البضاعة والفلوس في Sales و Purchase و Inventory و Accounting. بس العميل الحقيقي كمان بيصنّع، وبيجري ورا leads، وبيحاسب بالساعة، وبيقبّض موظفين، وعنده كاشيرات، وبيبيع online. مش مطلوب تبقى خبير في كل app، بس لازم تعرف الحقيقة متخزنة في أنهي model، وإمتى الـ module بيلمس الـ ledger، وأنهي واحد صعب لأسباب ملهاش علاقة بالكود.

كل اللي تحت اتشغّل على lab خاص بـ Odoo 20، إلا اللي مكتوب عليه "not run here". واتنين من الـ modules دول هيعلّموك أفكار هتستخدمها بره الـ ERP: الـ payroll هو المثال الكلاسيكي للـ effective-dated rules، والـ POS هو client حقيقي شغال offline-first وعنده conflict rules فعلية.

Part 1 — Manufacturing: BoMs, orders, work centers 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 1 — التصنيع: الـ BoM والـ manufacturing orders والـ work centers

English

Four models carry manufacturing. mrp.bom is the recipe: a finished product, its component lines and its operations. mrp.production is one manufacturing order (MO) that executes the recipe. mrp.workcenter is a place or machine with an hourly cost. mrp.workorder is one operation of one MO at one work center, and it records real time.

An MO is a stock document in disguise. action_confirm() creates the component moves and the work orders. action_assign() reserves components the way a delivery does (lesson 21). button_mark_done() consumes the components and produces the finished goods.

The cost rolls up from the bottom: components at their cost, plus operation minutes at the work center rate. In the run below that is 2 × 30 + (30 min × 60/hour) = 90. The finished move is worth +90 while the component move is −60, and the 30 of difference is labour absorbed into stock value. With valuation set to real_time no journal entry appeared at this step, matching what lesson 22 showed for receipts and deliveries.

A BoM has a type. normal means "Manufacture this product": it creates an MO and the finished product has its own stock. phantom means "Kit": no MO exists, and a sale explodes straight into component moves. Subcontracting adds a third value through the mrp_subcontracting module (not installed in the lab, so not run here): the BoM names subcontractor_ids, you send components to a partner, and receiving the finished goods closes an MO that ran at the partner's location.

# الإعداد: شركة مصر في الـ lab ملهاش warehouse، فلازم نعمل واحد الأول
from odoo import Command

co = env['res.company'].browse(4)
env.user.company_ids |= co

wh = e['stock.warehouse'].create({'name': 'Cairo WH', 'code': 'CAI', 'company_id': co.id})
loc = wh.lot_stock_id

def stock(product, quantity):
    e['stock.quant'].with_context(inventory_mode=True).create({
        'product_id': product.id, 'location_id': loc.id, 'inventory_quantity': quantity,
    }).action_apply_inventory()

def qty(product):
    return product.with_context(warehouse_id=wh.id).qty_available

print('bom types', e['mrp.bom']._fields['type'].selection)
cat = e['product.category'].create({'name': 'Mfg'})
cat.property_cost_method = 'fifo'
cat.property_valuation = 'real_time'

kit = e['product.product'].create({'name': 'Router Kit', 'is_storable': True, 'categ_id': cat.id, 'list_price': 200})

})
mo.action_confirm()
print('after confirm', mo.state, mo.workorder_ids.mapped('name'), 'expected', mo.workorder_ids.mapped('duration_expected'))
mo.action_assign()
print('reservation', mo.reservation_state)

# التنفيذ: الـ work order بيسجل الوقت، وبعدين الـ MO بيخلص

wo.button_finish()
mo.qty_producing = 1

finished = mo.move_finished_ids.filtered(lambda m: m.product_id == kit)
print('finished move value', finished.mapped('value'), 'raw value', mo.move_raw_ids.mapped('value'))

mouse = e['product.product'].create({'name': 'Mouse', 'is_storable': True, 'categ_id': cat.id, 'standard_price': 5})

kit_order.action_confirm()
picking = kit_order.picking_ids
print('kit picking products', [(m.product_id.name, m.product_uom_qty) for m in picking.move_ids])
for move in picking.move_ids:
    move.quantity = move.product_uom_qty
    move.picked = True

الرسمة بتمشي من فوق لتحت: الـ BoM هي الوصفة، ولما تأكد الـ MO بيتولد منه work orders مربوطة بـ work center ليه سعر بالساعة. الـ components بتخرج من المخزن بقيمة 60، والمنتج الجاهز بيدخل بقيمة 90، والفرق 30 هو الشغل اللي دخل في قيمة المخزون. تحت: النوع normal فيه MO وللمنتج stock خاص بيه، والنوع phantom مفيهوش MO والبيع بيتفتح على طول لـ moves للـ components.
بالعامية

التصنيع شايله 4 models. الـ mrp.bom هي الوصفة: منتج نهائي، وسطور الـ components، والـ operations. الـ mrp.production هو manufacturing order (MO) واحد بينفذ الوصفة. الـ mrp.workcenter مكان أو ماكينة ليها تكلفة بالساعة. والـ mrp.workorder هو operation واحدة من MO واحد على work center واحد، وهو اللي بيسجل الوقت الفعلي.

الـ MO في الحقيقة stock document متنكر. action_confirm() بتعمل moves الـ components والـ work orders. action_assign() بتحجز الـ components زي ما الـ delivery بتحجز (درس 21). وbutton_mark_done() بتستهلك الـ components وتطلّع المنتج الجاهز.

التكلفة بتطلع من تحت لفوق: الـ components بتكلفتها، زائد دقايق الـ operation بسعر الـ work center. هنا الحسبة 2 × 30 + (30 دقيقة × 60 في الساعة) = 90. الـ finished move قيمته +90 والـ component move قيمته −60، والـ 30 الفرق ده شغل دخل في قيمة المخزون. ومع valuation real_time مظهرش journal entry في الخطوة دي، وده نفس اللي شفناه للاستلام والتسليم في درس 22.

الـ BoM ليها type. normal يعني "Manufacture this product": بيتعمل MO والمنتج ليه stock. phantom يعني "Kit": مفيش MO، والبيع بيتفتح لـ moves للـ components على طول. والـ subcontracting بيزوّد قيمة تالتة عن طريق module mrp_subcontracting (مش متنزل في الـ lab، فده not run here): الـ BoM بتحدد subcontractor_ids، إنت بتبعت الـ components لشريك، ولما تستلم المنتج الجاهز بيتقفل MO كان شغال عند الشريك.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ لو بتربط متجر Node بـ Odoo وعندك "bundle" فيه 3 منتجات: لو عملته phantom، الـ stock اللي هتعرضه في الـ API لازم يتحسب من الـ components، ومتحاولش تعمل stock.quant للـ bundle نفسه. ولو الـ endpoint بتاعك بيرجّع "available" من جدول فيه stock الـ bundle بس، هتبيع حاجة مش موجودة. نفس الكلام لو الشركة بتجمّع قبل البيع: ساعتها normal والـ stock حقيقي.
Trade-off: Kit or real manufacture A kit is cheap to run: no MO, no work orders, and availability follows the components. It cannot hold assembled stock or absorb labour cost. Real manufacture gives you cost roll-up, traceability and a finished stock count, and it costs every order an extra document and a step on the shop floor.
الـ Kit رخيص في التشغيل: مفيش MO ولا work orders، والمتاح بيمشي مع الـ components. بس مينفعش يبقى عندك منه stock متجمّع، ومبيشيلش تكلفة شغل. التصنيع الحقيقي بيديك cost roll-up و traceability ورقم stock للمنتج الجاهز، وتمنه إن كل طلب بيبقى ليه document زيادة وخطوة زيادة في المصنع.

Part 2 — CRM: leads, opportunities, quotations 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 2 — الـ CRM: الـ leads والـ opportunities والـ quotations

English

One model, crm.lead, holds both leads and opportunities. The type field separates them: a lead is unqualified interest, and convert_opportunity(partner) turns it into an opportunity tied to a customer. stage_id points at a crm.stage record, so the pipeline is data that each company edits. action_new_quotation() comes from sale_crm and opens a sale.order form with the opportunity pre-filled.

The boundary with accounting is the useful fact here. In the run below, the lead, the conversion, the quotation, marking the deal Won and even confirming the sales order created zero account.move records. A CRM forecast is an opinion about the future. The ledger starts at the invoice (lesson 23).

If the client already lives in HubSpot or Salesforce, the first question is ownership: which system is the master for the customer record, and in which direction does each field flow? Two-way sync without an owner per field produces overwrite loops. Lesson 30 treats this properly.

# الإعداد نفس بتاع الجزء 1: e هو الـ env بتاع شركة مصر

بالعامية

model واحد، crm.lead، شايل الـ leads والـ opportunities مع بعض. الـ field type هو اللي بيفرق: الـ lead اهتمام لسه متأكدناش منه، وconvert_opportunity(partner) بتحوله لـ opportunity مربوطة بعميل. الـ stage_id بيشاور على record في crm.stage، يعني الـ pipeline نفسه data وكل شركة بتعدله. وaction_new_quotation() جاية من sale_crm وبتفتح form لـ sale.order والـ opportunity متعبية فيها.

الحد الفاصل مع المحاسبة هو المعلومة المفيدة هنا. في التشغيل ده: الـ lead، والتحويل، والـ quotation، وتعليم الصفقة Won، وحتى تأكيد الـ sales order، كلهم عملوا صفر account.move. الـ forecast في الـ CRM رأي عن المستقبل، والـ ledger بيبدأ عند الفاتورة (درس 23).

لو العميل شغال أصلًا على HubSpot أو Salesforce، أول سؤال هو الملكية: أنهي system هو الـ master لبيانات العميل، وكل field ماشي في أنهي اتجاه؟ two-way sync من غير owner لكل field بيعمل overwrite loops. درس 30 بيشرح ده بالتفصيل.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ فورم "Contact sales" في موقع Next: الـ route handler بيكتب crm.lead بـ type: 'lead' عن طريق JSON-2 (درس 27) بدل ما يبعت إيميل بيضيع. والداشبورد بتاعك لو بيعرض "revenue" متحسبهوش من expected_revenue أبدًا: ده رقم بيعبّيه الـ sales بإيده. الـ revenue الحقيقي من الفواتير المرحّلة.
Interview question "Marketing wants the CRM and the ERP to both edit customer addresses. What do you do?" → Name one owner per field, not per record. For example the ERP owns the legal name, tax ID and billing address because invoices depend on them, and the CRM owns contact people and marketing consent. Sync one way per field, and log rejected writes instead of silently overwriting.
"الـ marketing عايزين الـ CRM والـ ERP الاتنين يعدلوا عناوين العملاء. هتعمل إيه؟" ← حدد owner واحد لكل field، مش لكل record. مثلًا الـ ERP يملك الاسم القانوني والـ tax ID وعنوان الفوترة لأن الفواتير معتمدة عليهم، والـ CRM يملك الأشخاص وموافقات الـ marketing. الـ sync يبقى في اتجاه واحد لكل field، وأي write اترفض يتسجل في log بدل ما يعمل overwrite في السكوت.

Part 3 — Projects and timesheets: time is analytic data 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 3 — الـ Projects والـ timesheets: الوقت analytic data

English

project.project holds project.task records, and a timesheet is not its own model. It is a row in account.analytic.line with project_id, task_id, employee_id and unit_amount (the hours). task.effective_hours is a stored sum of those rows.

The model name is the lesson. Logging 2 hours for an employee costing 300 an hour produced an analytic line with amount = -600, no general account, no linked journal item, and zero account.move records. Time is analytic data: it tells you a project's profitability without changing the balance sheet. Salaries reach the ledger through payroll or a bill, not through timesheets.

For a software agency billing time and materials, the service product uses service_policy = 'delivered_timesheet' and invoice_policy = 'delivery'. Confirming the order creates the project and task (service_tracking = 'task_in_project'). Each timesheet on that task feeds qty_delivered, and the invoice bills delivered hours rather than the 40 sold. Once the invoice is posted, the timesheet is linked through reinvoice_move_id and Odoo refuses to edit it.

Part 4 — HR and payroll: why localization is hard 🔴 Advancedالجزء 4 — الـ HR والـ payroll: ليه الـ localization صعب

hr.employee ships in Community. hr.payslip and hr.salary.rule don't: in this checkout both are absent from the registry and there is no hr_payroll module, because Payroll is Enterprise. So payslip computation in Odoo is not run here. What Community does give you is the effective-dating primitive: an employee delegates to hr.version records, each with a date_version and a wage, and _get_version(date) answers "what was true on that day".

Payroll is hard for reasons that aren't technical. Rules differ by country, change by law, and take effect on dates that can be earlier than the date they were published. A correct system has to answer two separate questions: what rule applied on this date, and what did we know when we paid? When a change arrives late, you don't edit the payslip already paid and reported. You recompute it and carry the difference into the current period as a retroactive line.

The engine below applies that to an invented scheme. It is not the law of any country, and its rates are made up to make the arithmetic easy to follow. For real payroll, take each rate from a dated official source and have a local expert sign it off. The Odoo 20 release notes also say that salary rules "can now belong to multiple categories and be used in multiple structures": one rule definition can now serve several pay structures. That's an Enterprise feature, read from the notes and not run here.

// الـ rule set الصح هو آخر واحد بدأ قبل أو في التاريخ المطلوب
function ruleSetOn(history: readonly RuleSet[], on: IsoDate, knownAt?: IsoDate): RuleSet {
  const visible = knownAt ? history.filter((r) => r.publishedOn <= knownAt) : history;
  const found = visible
    .filter((r) => r.effectiveFrom <= on)
    .sort((a, b) => b.effectiveFrom.localeCompare(a.effectiveFrom))[0];
  if (!found) throw new Error(`no rule set in force on ${on}`);
  return found;
}

// تقريب نص لفوق على الـ integer، من غير floating point
const permille = (amount: Piastres, rate: number): Piastres => Math.floor((amount * rate + 500) / 1000);

function progressiveTax(taxable: Piastres, brackets: readonly Bracket[]): Piastres {
  let tax = 0;
  let floor = 0;
  for (const { upTo, ratePermille } of brackets) {
    const ceiling = upTo ?? Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY;
    const slice = Math.max(0, Math.min(taxable, ceiling) - floor);
    tax += permille(slice, ratePermille);
    floor = ceiling;
    if (taxable <= ceiling) break;
  }
  return tax;
}

const daysIn = (year: number, month: number): number => new Date(Date.UTC(year, month, 0)).getUTCDate();
const iso = (year: number, month: number, day: number): IsoDate =>
  `${year}-${String(month).padStart(2, "0")}-${String(day).padStart(2, "0")}` as IsoDate;

// المرتب بيتقسم بالأيام لو اتغير في نص الشهر
function grossFor(year: number, month: number, wages: readonly WageVersion[]): Piastres {
  const total = daysIn(year, month);
  const daysPerVersion = new Map<WageVersion, number>();
  for (let day = 1; day <= total; day++) {
    const on = iso(year, month, day);
    const version = wages
      .filter((w) => w.effectiveFrom <= on)
      .sort((a, b) => b.effectiveFrom.localeCompare(a.effectiveFrom))[0];
    if (version) daysPerVersion.set(version, (daysPerVersion.get(version) ?? 0) + 1);
  }
  let gross = 0;
  for (const [version, days] of daysPerVersion) {
    gross += Math.floor((version.monthly * days + Math.floor(total / 2)) / total);
  }
  return gross;
}

function computePayslip(
  year: number,
  month: number,
  wages: readonly WageVersion[],
  history: readonly RuleSet[],
  knownAt?: IsoDate,
): Payslip {
  const periodEnd = iso(year, month, daysIn(year, month));
  const rules = ruleSetOn(history, periodEnd, knownAt);
  const gross = grossFor(year, month, wages);
  const levy = Math.min(permille(gross, rules.levyPermille), rules.levyCap);
  const tax = progressiveTax(gross - levy, rules.brackets);
  return {
    period: `${year}-${String(month).padStart(2, "0")}`,
    ruleSetFrom: rules.effectiveFrom,
    lines: [
      { code: "GROSS", amount: gross },
      { code: "LEVY", amount: -levy },
      { code: "TAX", amount: -tax },
      { code: "NET", amount: gross - levy - tax },
    ],
  };
}

const netOf = (slip: Payslip): Piastres => slip.lines.find((l) => l.code === "NET")!.amount;

// الفرق بيتحط سطر في الشهر الحالي، والـ payslip القديم مبيتعدلش
function retroDelta(paid: Payslip, recomputed: Payslip): PayLine | null {
  const delta = netOf(recomputed) - netOf(paid);
  return delta === 0 ? null : { code: "RETRO", amount: delta, note: `re-rated ${paid.period}` };
}

Part 5 — POS: working offline and reaching the books 🔴 Advancedالجزء 5 — الـ POS: الشغل offline والوصول للدفاتر

English

The Odoo POS is a browser application. It loads products, prices and taxes once, then keeps working when the network drops. Its record layer in addons/point_of_sale/static/src/app/models/ persists to IndexedDB through utils/indexed_db.js. A paid order that hasn't synced stays there: canBeRemovedFromIndexedDB is false until the order is finalized and synced. When a push fails with ConnectionLostError, pos_store.js logs "Offline mode active, order will be synced later" and keeps the order pending.

Orders reach the server through pos.order.sync_from_ui, and the client generates the identifier. uuid is unique on pos.order, pos.order.line and pos.payment. The server looks the order up by uuid: not found means create, draft means update, anything else means the sync is ignored, with one exception for a paid order that hasn't been printed or invoiced. In that path _update_lines converts a create command into an update when the line's uuid already exists on that order.

The run below shows that the child identifiers are what make a replay safe. With a uuid on each line and payment, sending the same payload twice left one order, one line and one payment. Without them, the same order uuid still gave one order, but it now had 2 lines and 2 payments: 20 instead of 10. Idempotency on the parent alone is not idempotency.

At session close the books get a summary and not one entry per ticket. _create_session_account_move builds one document with a line per revenue account and VAT group, a line per tax, and a line per payment method. Then each payment method gets its own entry. Four tickets produced 3 journal entries, none of them before closing. Invoiced orders are the exception: they have their own invoice and stay out of the summary.

import { randomUUID } from "node:crypto";

interface Sale {
  uuid: string;
  sessionId: number;
  totalPiastres: number;
  state: "draft" | "paid";
}

// السيرفر: نفس فكرة sync_from_ui، upsert بالـ uuid
class Server {
  orders = new Map<string, Sale>();
  processed = 0;
  ignored = 0;
  sync(batch: readonly Sale[]): string[] {
    const acked: string[] = [];
    for (const sale of batch) {
      const existing = this.orders.get(sale.uuid);
      if (!existing || existing.state === "draft") {
        this.orders.set(sale.uuid, sale);
        this.processed++;
      } else {
        // الأوردر اتدفع خلاص، فالـ retry بيتجاهل ومبيتحسبش تاني
        this.ignored++;
      }
      acked.push(sale.uuid);
    }
    return acked;
  }
}

type Transport = (batch: readonly Sale[]) => Promise<string[]>;

// الـ outbox: في المتصفح ده IndexedDB، هنا Map عشان يتشغل على Node
class Outbox {
  pending = new Map<string, Sale>();
  private readonly send: Transport;
  constructor(send: Transport) {
    this.send = send;
  }

  record(sessionId: number, totalPiastres: number): Sale {
    const sale: Sale = { uuid: randomUUID(), sessionId, totalPiastres, state: "paid" };
    this.pending.set(sale.uuid, sale);
    return sale;
  }

  async flush(): Promise<"ok" | "offline"> {
    if (this.pending.size === 0) return "ok";
    try {
      const acked = await this.send([...this.pending.values()]);
      for (const uuid of acked) this.pending.delete(uuid);
      return "ok";
    } catch {
      return "offline";
    }
  }
}

const server = new Server();
let mode: "down" | "lost-ack" | "up" = "down";

const transport: Transport = async (batch) => {
  if (mode === "down") throw new Error("network down");
  const acked = server.sync(batch);
  // السيرفر كتب، بس الرد ضاع في السكة
  if (mode === "lost-ack") throw new Error("response lost");
  return acked;
};

Part 6 — Website and eCommerce 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 6 — الـ Website والـ eCommerce

English

website gives you pages and a builder, and website_sale adds the shop. In the lab GET /shop returned 200. The important design fact is that the cart isn't a separate model: /shop/cart/add created a sale.order in state draft with website_id set, belonging to the Public user until checkout identifies someone. A cart is an unconfirmed quotation.

The same-database shop therefore has no sync at all. Prices come from the pricelist, stock comes from the warehouse and taxes come from the fiscal position in the same transaction. There are no webhooks, no duplicate orders and no stale catalog. The cost is that your storefront is a QWeb and Python application, served by the same workers that post invoices (lesson 18 covers the frontend side).

A Next.js storefront calling JSON-2 (lesson 27) reverses every one of those points. You gain control over rendering, caching and SEO (section 08) and a stack your team already knows. You take on a sync problem, because price, stock and order creation now cross a network that can fail. Keep the API key on the server, never in the browser. Also check the hosting: per the docs, the external API on Odoo Online needs the Custom plan. Neither option was benchmarked here.

Trade-off: decide by who pays for the sync A separate storefront is justified by traffic that would hurt back-office workers, by a brand experience QWeb can't deliver, or by a team that already owns a frontend. Without one of those, you are buying a distributed system to change how a page looks.

Solution · الحل 
// الدالة دي بتتضاف في آخر payroll-engine.ts
function retroRun(
  periods: readonly [number, number][],
  wages: readonly WageVersion[],
  history: readonly RuleSet[],
  paidAt: (year: number, month: number) => IsoDate,
  knownAt: IsoDate,
): Piastres {
  let total = 0;
  for (const [year, month] of periods) {
    const paid = computePayslip(year, month, wages, history, paidAt(year, month));
    const now = computePayslip(year, month, wages, history, knownAt);
    total += retroDelta(paid, now)?.amount ?? 0;
  }
  return total;
}

// الاتنين اتدفعوا قبل ما القانون يتنشر يوم 15 أغسطس

interface Line {
  uuid: string;
  productId: number;
  qty: number;
}

// غلط: كل إرسال بيضيف السطور فوق اللي موجود
function appendLines(stored: Line[], incoming: readonly Line[]): Line[] {
  return [...stored, ...incoming];
}

// صح: السطر بيتعرف بالـ uuid، فالـ replay بيكتب فوق نفسه
function mergeLines(stored: Line[], incoming: readonly Line[]): Line[] {
  const byId = new Map(stored.map((line) => [line.uuid, line]));
  for (const line of incoming) byId.set(line.uuid, line);
  return [...byId.values()];
}

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • mrp.bom recipe → mrp.production order → mrp.workorder at a mrp.workcenter. Confirm, assign, mark done.
  • Cost roll-up = components + (minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate). button_bom_cost() computes it.
  • phantom kit: no MO, no own stock, availability derives from components.
  • crm.lead is both lead and opportunity. No journal entry until an invoice.
  • A timesheet is an account.analytic.line. It measures profitability and doesn't touch the ledger.
  • Time and materials: delivered_timesheet bills delivered hours. Invoiced timesheets are locked.
  • hr.employee and hr.version are Community. hr.payslip is Enterprise.
  • Payroll needs two time axes: effective date and what was known when paid. Corrections are retro lines.
  • POS: IndexedDB plus sync_from_ui, upsert by client uuid on order, lines and payments.
  • POS books: nothing until session close, then a summary plus one entry per payment method.
  • The web cart is a draft sale.order. Same database means no sync; Next.js means you own the sync.
