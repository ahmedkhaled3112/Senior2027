Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

Every integration you will write against Odoo ends at one table pair: account_move and account_move_line. An order sync, a payment webhook and an e-invoice all read or create rows there. If you treat an invoice like a plain document with a total, you will write code that passes in a demo and corrupts the books in production: taxes posted to the income account, a total changed on a posted invoice, an export charged 14% VAT.

This lesson opens the model and shows what it really does, using a real Odoo 20 lab. Several results differ from older tutorials, and a few differ from what you would guess. Every output below is copied from a run on the Egyptian demo company.

Part 1 — account.move from the inside 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 1 — account.move من جوه

English

account.move is one model for customer invoices, vendor bills, manual journal entries, and the entries behind payments and bank lines. A single field discriminates them: move_type, with seven values: entry, out_invoice, out_refund, in_invoice, in_refund, out_receipt, in_receipt. state is draft, posted or cancel.

The move has two views over the same lines. line_ids is every accounting line. invoice_line_ids is the subset a person edits on the invoice form. display_type says what each line is: product, tax, payment_term (the receivable or payable line), rounding, discount, epd (early payment discount), cogs, the non-accounting line_section, line_subsection and line_note, and three non-deductible types.

Tax and receivable lines are dynamic: you don't write them. _sync_dynamic_lines wraps create and write, and after your change it recalculates what those lines must be and creates, updates or deletes them. So the invoice form is really a projection, and the ledger lines are derived from it.

بالعامية

بص، account.move ده model واحد شايل كل حاجة: فواتير العملاء، فواتير الموردين، القيود اليدوية، والقيود اللي ورا الدفعات وسطور البنك. اللي بيفرّق بينهم حقل واحد اسمه move_type وليه 7 قيم: entry وout_invoice وout_refund وin_invoice وin_refund وout_receipt وin_receipt. والـ state يا draft يا posted يا cancel.

عندك نظرتين على نفس السطور. line_ids فيها كل سطر محاسبي. invoice_line_ids فيها الجزء اللي البني آدم بيعدّله في الفورم. وdisplay_type بيقولك كل سطر إيه: product، tax، payment_term (ده سطر الـ receivable أو الـ payable)، rounding، discount، epd (خصم الدفع المبكر)، cogs، والسطور اللي ملهاش قيمة محاسبية line_section وline_subsection وline_note، وتلات أنواع للـ non-deductible.

سطور الضريبة والـ receivable dynamic: إنت مبتكتبهاش. _sync_dynamic_lines بيلف حوالين create وwrite، وبعد أي تعديل بيحسب السطور دي المفروض تبقى إيه وبيعملها أو يعدّلها أو يمسحها. يعني الفورم هو الأصل، وسطور الدفاتر متشتقة منه.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ لما تعمل sync لفاتورة من الـ Node API بتاعك، ابعت invoice_line_ids بس (منتج وكمية وسعر وضرايب) وسيب Odoo يطلّع سطور الضريبة والـ receivable. لو بعتّ line_ids كاملة بأرقام محسوبة عندك، أول تعديل على الفاتورة هيعيد الحساب ويختلف عن رقمك. الغلطة الشائعة: تحفظ amount_total من الـ API عندك وأنت لسه في draft، وبعدين تتفاجئ إن الرقم اتغير.

Part 2 — Balance and currency columns 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 2 — أعمدة الـ balance والعملة

English

Five amount columns matter on a line. balance is the signed value in company currency, positive for debit. debit and credit are stored but computed from balance; a write to them is turned into a write to balance by _sanitize_vals. amount_currency is the signed value in the document's currency. amount_residual is what is still open after reconciliation, in company currency, with amount_residual_currency as its twin.

Postgres enforces four CHECK constraints. One of them, account_move_line_check_amount_currency_balance_sign, says balance and amount_currency can never have opposite signs. It does not enforce that the entry balances: that rule lives in Python, in _check_balanced, and the table has no triggers.

Now the surprise. What a direct write to a posted line does depends on the currency. On a foreign-currency invoice, balance moves while amount_currency doesn't, the signs disagree, and the database stops it with CheckViolation. On a company-currency invoice, _sync_invoice copies balance into amount_currency, the receivable line is re-derived, and the write succeeds: the posted invoice's total changes from 400 to 300. Lesson 24 tests this as a normal accountant and shows the controls that close it.

def attempt(label, make, vals, line_type='product'):
    try:
        with env.cr.savepoint():
            inv = make()
            inv.action_post()
            inv.line_ids.filtered(lambda l: l.display_type == line_type).write(vals)
            env.flush_all()
            print(f'{label}: ALLOWED, total now {inv.amount_total}')
            show(inv)
            raise Undo()
    except Undo:
        pass
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f'{label}: {type(ex).__name__}: {str(ex).splitlines()[0].split("constraint ")[-1][:80]}')
    env.invalidate_all()

بالعامية

فيه 5 أعمدة مبالغ مهمين على السطر. balance القيمة بإشارتها بعملة الشركة، موجب يعني مدين. debit وcredit متخزنين بس محسوبين من balance، ولو كتبت فيهم _sanitize_vals بيحوّلها كتابة على balance. amount_currency القيمة بإشارتها بعملة المستند. amount_residual اللي لسه مفتوح بعد الـ reconciliation بعملة الشركة، وأخوه amount_residual_currency بعملة المستند.

Postgres بيفرض 4 CHECK constraints. واحد منهم account_move_line_check_amount_currency_balance_sign بيقول إن balance وamount_currency عمرهم ما يبقوا بإشارتين مختلفتين. بس هو مش بيضمن إن القيد متوازن: القاعدة دي في Python جوه _check_balanced، والجدول مفيهوش triggers.

وهنا المفاجأة. الكتابة المباشرة على سطر posted نتيجتها بتعتمد على العملة. في فاتورة بعملة أجنبية، balance بيتحرك وamount_currency لأ، الإشارتين بيختلفوا، والـ database بتوقفها بـ CheckViolation. في فاتورة بعملة الشركة، _sync_invoice بينسخ balance في amount_currency، وسطر الـ receivable بيتحسب من جديد، والكتابة بتنجح: total فاتورة posted بيتغير من 400 لـ 300. درس 24 بيجرب ده بمحاسب عادي ويوريك الـ controls اللي بتقفله.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ لو بتكتب data migration أو script تصحيح بـ raw SQL على account_move_line، الـ database مش هتحميك من قيد مش متوازن: اكتب بعدها query بتتأكد إن SUM(balance) = 0 لكل move_id. ولو عندك endpoint بيعمل write على سطور القيد عن طريق JSON-2، امنعه: الصح هو reversal. الغلطة الشائعة إنك تفترض إن كلمة posted معناها الـ ORM هيرفض أي تعديل.
What breaks in production A correction script that writes balance on a posted company-currency invoice gets no error. Part 4 — The tax engine 🔴 Advancedالجزء 4 — محرك الضرايب

English

An account.tax has an amount_type: percent (100 × 14% = 14), fixed, division (a percentage of the final price: 100 / (1 − 14%) = 116.28), or group, which holds children_tax_ids. price_include_override set to tax_included makes the entered price tax-inclusive. include_base_amount adds this tax into the base of later taxes that have is_base_affected.

compute_all(price, quantity=...) is the engine's public face. It returns total_excluded, total_included, total_void, base_tags and a taxes list; each tax entry carries amount, base, account_id and the tax_repartition_line_id that says where it posts.

Repartition lines are where a tax meets the chart. Each tax has base and tax lines for invoices and for refunds, and the tax line names the account (220300 here). A tax created from nothing has none, so its amount falls on the income account: the invoice still looks right and the VAT report is wrong. Rounding is a company setting, tax_calculation_rounding_method: per line or global. Three lines of 0.33 at 14% give 0.15 against 0.14. Money rules are in lesson 04.

type Line = { unitMinor: bigint; qty: bigint };
type Mode = "round_per_line" | "round_globally";

const BP = 10_000n;

// التقريب half-up على integers: بنزوّد نص المقام قبل القسمة
const roundDiv = (n: bigint, d: bigint): bigint => (n + d / 2n) / d;

function taxOf(lines: Line[], rateBp: bigint, mode: Mode): bigint {
  const raw = lines.map((l) => l.unitMinor * l.qty * rateBp);
  if (mode === "round_per_line") {
    // كل سطر بيتقرّب لوحده وبعدين بنجمع
    return raw.reduce((sum, r) => sum + roundDiv(r, BP), 0n);
  }
  // بنجمع الأول بالدقة الكاملة وبنقرّب مرة واحدة في الآخر
  return roundDiv(raw.reduce((sum, r) => sum + r, 0n), BP);
}

Part 5 — Fiscal positions 🔴 Advancedالجزء 5 — الـ fiscal positions

English

A fiscal position changes taxes and accounts according to who the customer is. _get_fiscal_position first honours a position set by hand on the partner (property_account_position_id), then looks through auto_apply positions for one matching the delivery country.

Odoo 20 moved the mapping. Older docs show a table of source and destination taxes on the position. Here the link lives on the tax: fiscal_position_ids says which positions a tax belongs to, original_tax_ids says which domestic taxes it replaces, and replacing_tax_ids is the reverse. tax_map on the position is a computed JSON, and map_tax reads it. Part 6 — Invoices, credit notes, bills 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 6 — الفواتير والـ credit notes والـ bills

English

Four types carry most of the traffic. out_invoice debits receivable and credits income and tax. out_refund is its mirror. in_invoice debits expense and input tax and credits payable. in_refund mirrors that. Numbers are assigned at posting by sequence.mixin, in posting order and not creation order, so a draft has no number and deleting drafts leaves no gaps. See lesson 20 for sequences.

_reverse_moves creates a draft refund linked by reversed_entry_id. Posting it reconciles it with the original automatically, so a full credit makes the invoice reversed, and a partial credit leaves it partial with the remainder open. The wizard account.move.reversal has two buttons over one method, reverse_moves: refund_moves leaves a draft refund, and modify_moves posts the reversal and opens a fresh draft copy to correct. A debit note (account_debit_note) is an out_invoice with debit_origin_id pointing at the original, numbered with a D prefix.

A bill from a purchase order links each line through purchase_line_id. With purchase_method='receive' the billable quantity comes from what arrived, which is the quantity leg of the 3-way match from lesson 12. The price leg is weaker: in the lab a bill at 650 against a PO at 600 posted with no block, and a duplicate vendor reference raised only a warning field.

4 أنواع شايلين معظم الشغل. out_invoice مدين الـ receivable ودائن الإيراد والضريبة. out_refund عكسه. in_invoice مدين المصروف وضريبة المدخلات ودائن الـ payable. in_refund عكسه. الأرقام بتتاخد وقت الـ post عن طريق sequence.mixin، بترتيب الـ post مش الإنشاء، فالـ draft ملوش رقم ومسح الـ drafts مبيسيبش فجوات. الـ sequences بالتفصيل في درس 20.

_reverse_moves بيعمل refund draft مربوط بـ reversed_entry_id. لما تعمله post بيتعمل reconcile مع الأصل لوحده، فالـ credit الكامل بيخلي الفاتورة reversed، والجزئي بيسيبها partial والباقي مفتوح. الـ wizard account.move.reversal ليه زرارين فوق method واحدة اسمها reverse_moves: refund_moves بيسيب refund draft، وmodify_moves بيعمل post للعكس ويفتح نسخة draft جديدة تصلّح فيها. الـ debit note (account_debit_note) هي out_invoice فيها debit_origin_id بيشاور على الأصل ورقمها بيبدأ بحرف D.

الـ bill اللي جاي من purchase order كل سطر فيه مربوط بـ purchase_line_id. ومع purchase_method='receive' الكمية القابلة للفوترة بتيجي من اللي وصل فعلًا، وده جزء الكمية في الـ 3-way match من درس 12. جزء السعر أضعف: في الـ lab، bill بسعر 650 قصاد PO بـ 600 اتعمله post من غير أي منع، والـ vendor reference المكرر طلّع حقل تحذير بس.

فين هستخدمه في شغلي؟ زرار "Refund" في الـ admin panel بتاعك المفروض يعمل _reverse_moves ويعمل post، مش يعدّل الفاتورة ولا يمسحها. وفي pipeline بيستقبل فواتير موردين، Odoo مش هيحميك: اعمل UNIQUE على (vendor, ref) عندك في الـ Node service قبل ما تبعت، وقارن السعر بالـ PO وارفع تنبيه لو الفرق أكبر من tolerance. الغلطة الشائعة: تعمل reconcile بإيدك بعد الـ post وأنت Odoo عملها لوحده.

Solution · الحل 
// مرآة صغيرة للـ compute_all في Node: percent بس، excluded و included و group
type Tax = { name: string; bp: bigint; included?: boolean };
const BP = 10_000n;
// القسمة في BigInt بتقطع ناحية الصفر، فالسالب لازم يتقرّب من ناحية الموجب
const round = (n: bigint, d: bigint): bigint => (n >= 0n ? (n + d / 2n) / d : -((-n + d / 2n) / d));

function computeAll(priceMinor: bigint, taxes: Tax[]) {
  // السعر شامل: بنطلّع الأساس بالقسمة على مجموع النسب المشمولة
  const includedBp = taxes.filter((t) => t.included).reduce((s, t) => s + t.bp, 0n);
  const base = round(priceMinor * BP, BP + includedBp);
  const lines = taxes.map((t) => ({ name: t.name, amount: round(base * t.bp, BP) }));
  const total = base + lines.reduce((s, l) => s + l.amount, 0n);
  return { excluded: base, included: total, lines };
}

const wht: Tax = { name: "WHT 1", bp: -100n };

console.log(computeAll(11_400n, [{ ...vat, included: true }]));
console.log(computeAll(10_000n, [vat, wht]));

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • account.move is one model for all entries, split by move_type (7 values); line_ids is everything, invoice_line_ids is what people edit.
  • Tax and payment_term lines are derived by _sync_dynamic_lines: send product lines and let Odoo build the rest.
  • debit and credit come from balance; amount_currency is the document currency; amount_residual is what's open.
  • Postgres has 4 row CHECKs and 0 triggers on the lines. Balance is enforced in Python only.
  • Writing balance on a posted company-currency invoice succeeds and changes its total; on a foreign-currency one it hits CheckViolation.
  • Numbering happens at posting: yearly for sale and bank journals, monthly otherwise, R for refunds, P for payments.
  • Odoo 20 has no account.group: use parent_id; parents are inactive headers.
  • compute_all shows the engine; repartition lines decide the account; a bare tax posts to income.
  • Rounding per line against global: 0.15 against 0.14 on 3 × 0.33 at 14%.
  • Fiscal mapping lives on the tax now. An unmapped tax tied to another position is removed. After changing a partner in code, call action_update_fpos_values().
  • _reverse_moves makes a draft that auto-reconciles on posting. Duplicate vendor refs and price differences don't block posting.

  • account.move model واحد لكل القيود، بيتقسم بـ move_type (7 قيم). line_ids فيها كل حاجة وinvoice_line_ids اللي الناس بتعدّله.
  • سطور الضريبة وpayment_term بيشتقها _sync_dynamic_lines: ابعت سطور المنتجات وسيب Odoo يكمّل.
