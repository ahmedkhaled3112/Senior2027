Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

You can spend months preparing for the loop and then lose much of what it was worth in one phone call, because you answered "what are your expectations?" with the first number that came to mind. Negotiation is a small, teachable skill built on preparation: knowing your range, comparing whole packages over years instead of one base figure, and answering honestly when you're asked about other offers. It also shapes the working relationship you'll start with, so doing it respectfully matters as much as doing it at all.

بالعامية

ممكن تقضي شهور تذاكر للـ loop، وبعدين تضيّع جزء كبير من قيمته في مكالمة تليفون واحدة، عشان جاوبت على "توقعاتك إيه؟" بأول رقم جه في دماغك. الـ negotiation مهارة صغيرة بتتعلم، ومبنية على التحضير: تعرف الـ range بتاعك، تقارن العروض كاملة على سنين مش رقم base واحد، وترد بأمانة لما يسألوك عن عروض تانية. وكمان هي اللي بتحدد شكل العلاقة اللي هتبدأ بيها الشغل، فإنك تعملها باحترام مهم زي إنك تعملها أصلًا.

Part 1 — Finding your range 🔴 Advancedالجزء 1 — تعرف الـ range بتاعك

⏱ Timed prompt: 15 minutes Fill the worksheet below from real data for your own city and your own level: at least five points, each with its source, date, level and location, and remote and local kept apart. Stop at 15 minutes and note what you couldn't find.
⏱ تمرين بتوقيت: 15 دقيقة. املا الـ worksheet اللي تحت بـ data حقيقية عن مدينتك والـ level بتاعك: 5 نقط على الأقل، كل واحدة بمصدرها وتاريخها والـ level والمكان، و remote منفصل عن local. وقّف عند 15 دقيقة واكتب اللي مقدرتش تلاقيه.
English

Four sources, each with a weakness. levels.fyi and Glassdoor are self-reported, and how much data exists depends heavily on your city and on which companies are in it. Your network is the most relevant source and the smallest: ask two or three people you trust for a range rather than an exact figure, and offer yours in return. Recruiters know the current budget for the role, and a good first question is "what range has been budgeted for this?". Look these up for your own city. When this lesson was checked, levels.fyi's location pages refused automated requests, so this page doesn't describe what they contain, and you'll have to read them yourself.

Remote and local are separate ranges. A local role, a remote role paid from abroad and a role through an employer of record can differ in currency and in terms, and the tax and employment rules for each are something to check locally. Never average them together.

Three checks on every data point. Does its level match yours? (A title called "Senior" in one company can be a mid-level role in another.) Is it recent? Is the sample big enough? A range built from a single colleague's offer two years ago isn't a range.

The result is a floor, a target and a stretch for you specifically, written down before any recruiter call. Whatever you say as the bottom of a range is what they will hear, so the bottom has to be a figure you'd truly accept.

// الأداة دي بتنضّف نقط الـ data بتاعة الـ range قبل ما تبني عليها رقم.
// للتشغيل: node range.ts
// كل الأرقام هنا وحدات وهمية ⁦normalized (⁩أساس العرض A بيساوي 100)، مش مرتبات حقيقية.
type Source = "levels.fyi" | "glassdoor" | "network" | "recruiter";
type Point = { source: Source; date: string; level: string; location: "local" | "remote"; total: number };

const AS_OF = new Date("2026-11-09");
const MAX_AGE_MONTHS = 12;
const MIN_SAMPLE = 5;
const TARGET_LEVEL = "senior";

const monthsOld = (d: string): number => {
  const then = new Date(d);
  return (AS_OF.getFullYear() - then.getFullYear()) * 12 + AS_OF.getMonth() - then.getMonth();
};

const median = (xs: number[]): number => {
  const s = xs.toSorted((a, b) => a - b);
  const mid = Math.floor(s.length / 2);
  return s.length % 2 ? s[mid] : (s[mid - 1] + s[mid]) / 2;
};

export function summarize(points: Point[], location: Point["location"]): string[] {
  const log: string[] = [`--- ${location}`];
  const kept: Point[] = [];
  for (const p of points.filter((x) => x.location === location)) {
    // النقطة القديمة أو بتاعة level تاني بتشد الـ range من غير ما تحس
    if (monthsOld(p.date) > MAX_AGE_MONTHS) log.push(`  drop ${p.source} ${p.date}: older than ${MAX_AGE_MONTHS} months`);
    else if (p.level !== TARGET_LEVEL) log.push(`  drop ${p.source} ${p.date}: level is ${p.level}`);
    else kept.push(p);
  }
  if (!kept.length) return [...log, "  nothing usable"];
  const totals = kept.map((p) => p.total);
  const [min, max] = [Math.min(...totals), Math.max(...totals)];
  log.push(`  kept ${kept.length}: min ${min}, median ${median(totals)}, max ${max}, spread ${max - min} units`);
  if (kept.length < MIN_SAMPLE) log.push(`  warn: only ${kept.length} points, treat as a rough range`);
  const bySource = new Set(kept.map((p) => p.source));
  if (bySource.size < 2) log.push("  warn: one source only, ask a person too");
  return log;
}

Part 2 — Total compensation 🔴 Advancedالجزء 2 — الـ total comp

⏱ Timed prompt: 10 minutes Take three real or imagined offers and model them in total-comp.ts. Before you run it, write down which offer you expect to win. After it runs, explain why it did or didn't.
⏱ تمرين بتوقيت: 10 دقايق. خد 3 عروض حقيقية أو متخيلة وحطهم في total-comp.ts. قبل التشغيل اكتب أي عرض متوقع يكسب. وبعده اشرح ليه كسب أو ليه لأ.
English

Model solution. Base is one line out of several. A full comparison includes base, bonus, equity, signing, remote and learning budget, and it has to be taken across the time you expect to stay.

  • Bonus. A "target" is not a promise, so multiply it by a probability you can defend: ask how often it was paid in full over recent years.
  • Equity. Vesting and the cliff decide how much is yours and when: leave before the cliff and you get nothing. Treat a private company's equity as possibly worthless, and ask what class of shares it is and how you could ever sell it.
  • Signing. Often the easiest to move because it's a one-off, but check for a clawback if you leave early.
  • Remote. Worth real money: commute, time, moving. Model it as a value rather than ignoring it.
  • Learning budget. Small on paper and valuable to you if it gets used. Ask whether people actually use it.
  • Currency. If you're paid in another currency than the one you spend in, the exchange rate is a variable, not a fact. Test it in both directions.

What can usually be moved. Signing, equity, base within the band, level or title, start date, remote days, learning budget, relocation, review timing, and non-monetary things like team or first project. Which of these moves depends entirely on the company. Haseeb Qureshi lists "how much training you get, what your first project will be, which team you join, or even who your mentor will be" among the things you can and should negotiate.

// الموديل ده بيقارن العروض على كذا سنة، مش على الـ base لوحده.
// للتشغيل: node total-comp.ts
// كل الأرقام وحدات وهمية ⁦normalized (⁩أساس العرض A بيساوي 100)، والعروض كلها خيالية.
import { fileURLToPath } from "node:url";

export type Offer = {
  name: string;
  base: number;
  bonusTarget: number;
  bonusProbability: number;
  equityGrant: number;
  vestMonths: number;
  cliffMonths: number;
  signing: number;
  signingClawbackMonths: number;
  learningPerYear: number;
  commutePerYear: number;
  // جزء الكاش اللي بيتدفع بعملة غير اللي بتصرف بيها
  fxShare: number;
};

export type Scenario = { months: number; equityFactor: number; fx: number; bonusFactor: number };

export function totalComp(o: Offer, s: Scenario): number {
  const years = s.months / 12;
  const expectedBonus = o.bonusTarget * o.bonusProbability * s.bonusFactor;
  // جزء الكاش اللي بعملة تانية بيتضرب في الـ fx، ودي لازم تبقى متغير مش افتراض
  const cash = (o.base + expectedBonus) * (1 - o.fxShare + o.fxShare * s.fx);
  const vested = s.months < o.cliffMonths ? 0 : o.equityGrant * Math.min(s.months, o.vestMonths) / o.vestMonths;
  const signing = s.months >= o.signingClawbackMonths ? o.signing : 0;
  return (cash + o.learningPerYear - o.commutePerYear) * years + vested * s.equityFactor + signing;
}

بالعامية

الإجابة النموذجية. الـ base سطر واحد من كذا سطر. المقارنة الكاملة بتشمل base و bonus و equity و signing و remote و learning budget، ولازم تتحسب على المدة اللي متوقع تقعدها.

  • الـ bonus. الـ "target" مش وعد، فاضربه في احتمال تقدر تدافع عنه: اسأل اتدفع كامل كام مرة في السنين اللي فاتت.
  • الـ equity. الـ vesting والـ cliff بيحددوا إيه اللي بتاعك وإمتى: لو مشيت قبل الـ cliff مش هتاخد حاجة. واعتبر equity الشركات الخاصة ممكن تطلع بصفر، واسأل هي أي نوع من الأسهم وإزاي ممكن تبيعها أصلًا.
  • الـ signing. غالبًا أسهل حاجة تتحرك لأنها مرة واحدة، بس اتأكد من الـ clawback لو مشيت بدري.
  • الـ remote. ليه قيمة حقيقية: مواصلات، وقت، سكن. احسبه كقيمة بدل ما تتجاهله.
  • الـ learning budget. صغير على الورق ومفيد ليك لو بيتصرف فعلًا. اسأل الناس بتستخدمه ولا لأ.
  • العملة. لو بتقبض بعملة غير اللي بتصرف بيها، سعر الصرف متغير مش حقيقة. جرّبه في الاتجاهين.

إيه اللي غالبًا بيتحرك. الـ signing، الـ equity، الـ base جوه الـ band، الـ level أو اللقب، ميعاد البداية، أيام الـ remote، الـ learning budget، الانتقال، ميعاد الـ review، وحاجات مش فلوس زي الفريق أو أول مشروع. إيه اللي بيتحرك بيعتمد تمامًا على الشركة. و Haseeb Qureshi بيقول إن قد إيه هتتدرب، وأول مشروع ليك، والفريق اللي هتدخله، أو حتى الـ mentor، كلها حاجات تقدر ولازم تتفاوض عليها.

شغّل الموديل اللي فوق. كل عرض من التلاتة بيكسب في سيناريو معقول مختلف، وعشان كده رقم إجمالي واحد مش كفاية، والافتراضات أهم من الحساب.

Part 3 — Who says a number first 🔴 Advancedالجزء 3 — مين يقول الرقم الأول

⏱ Timed prompt: 10 minutes, role-play Get a friend to play the recruiter. They ask, in this order: "What are your salary expectations?", "What are you currently making?", "We need a number before we can move forward", "Is that your final number?". Answer out loud, recorded. Have your worksheet in front of you.
⏱ تمرين بتوقيت: 10 دقايق role-play. خلّي صاحب يلعب دور الـ recruiter، ويسأل بالترتيب ده: "توقعاتك للمرتب كام؟"، "بتاخد كام دلوقتي؟"، "لازم رقم عشان نكمل"، "ده رقمك النهائي؟". جاوب بصوت عالي ومتسجل، والـ worksheet قدامك.
English

Advice. Defer if you can; if you can't, give a range built from your data. The README's rule is a sensible default and not a law. Haseeb Qureshi's "Ten Rules" (2016) describes most writing on the subject as "a vague and long-winded exhortation to 'make sure you negotiate' and 'never say the first number'", and focuses instead on protecting information, keeping options open and giving reasons. So the skill is not refusing forever. It's not giving away your position before you know the role and the level.

How to defer. Ask to learn about the role and the level first, and ask for their budget. If they insist, give a range you'd be happy with at the bottom, based on your data, stated as the whole package.

"What's your current salary?" Whether an employer may ask at all, and what happens if you decline, differs by country and even by state or region, so check the rules where you live before the call. Haseeb's position is that you shouldn't reveal it as a rule; he adds that if you must, you should give the total value of your package and add that you're looking for a step up. A neutral reply is to talk about what you're looking for rather than what you make.

❌ Weak reply

"Sorry, I currently make 100 and I just want 120 if that's ok."

✅ Strong reply

"I'd like to understand the role and the level first, so I can give you a useful answer. What range did you budget for this role, as a total package?"

And if they insist: "Based on my research for this level and location, I'm looking at [LOW] to [HIGH] as a total package, depending on the bonus and equity."

// للتشغيل: node reply-lint.ts
// مؤشر تقريبي: بيدوّر على عبارات، ومبيعرفش سوقك ولا قوانين بلدك.
// الأرقام في الأمثلة وحدات وهمية ⁦normalized (⁩أساس العرض A بيساوي 100).
export function lintReply(text: string): string[] {
  const findings: string[] = [];
  if (/\b(currently (make|earn|get)|my current (salary|package|pay)( is|:))\b/i.test(text)) {
    findings.push("discloses current pay: that sets the anchor for you");
  }
  const hasRange = /\b(between|range|\bto\b.*\[HIGH\])/i.test(text);
  const hasBasis = /\b(based on|research|data points?|for this level)\b/i.test(text);
  const hasNumber = /\b\d{2,}\b|\[LOW\]/.test(text);
  if (hasNumber && !hasRange) findings.push("a single number: give a range");
  if (hasNumber && !hasBasis) findings.push("a number with no basis: say where it comes from");
  if (!/\b(total|package|whole offer|overall)\b/i.test(text)) findings.push("talks about base only: name the whole package");
  if (/\b(sorry|just|hopefully|if that('s| is) ok)\b/i.test(text)) findings.push("apologetic wording");
  return findings;
}

Part 4 — Beyond money 🔴 Advancedالجزء 4 — ما بعد الفلوس

⏱ Timed prompt: 10 minutes Take the answers you collected when you asked your own questions at the end of your interviews, and score each offer from 1 to 5 on team, growth, tech, on-call and culture. Then pick your weights before you see any totals, so the result can't steer you.
⏱ تمرين بتوقيت: 10 دقايق. هات الإجابات اللي جمعتها لما سألت أسئلتك في آخر الـ interviews، وادّي كل عرض درجة من 1 لـ 5 على الفريق، الـ growth، الـ tech، الـ on-call، والـ culture. وبعدين حدد الأوزان قبل ما تشوف أي إجمالي، عشان النتيجة متأثرش عليك.
English

The data comes from the questions you asked. Lesson 11 covers the questions to ask on on-call, tech debt, how decisions get made, review culture and the growth path. If you never asked, you don't have the data, so go back and ask before signing: "How often were you paged last month?", "Who decides when you pay down tech debt?". A company that won't answer has told you something.

A weighted matrix forces the trade-off into the open. Everyone's weights differ, so write yours down before scoring: a parent of small children and someone looking for a fast path to Staff shouldn't have the same on-call weight. Scores are still judgments, so don't believe the second decimal.

The useful output isn't the winner, it's what flips it. For each weight the script finds the smallest change that makes a different offer win. If a tiny change to one weight flips the decision, then you aren't deciding with the matrix: you need more information about that one thing, or you need to decide which you really care about. Below, the decision flips if Mariam cares about growth less than she thinks (0.20 → 0.08), but it would take doubling the weight she gives to pay to change it.

// المصفوفة دي بتقارن العروض بأوزان، وبتقولك أي وزن يقلب القرار.
// للتشغيل: node offer-score.ts
// الدرجات والأوزان خيالية: في الحقيقة الدرجة من 1 لـ 5 بتيجي من إجاباتهم على أسئلتك.
type Criterion = "pay" | "team" | "growth" | "tech" | "onCall" | "culture";
type Weights = Record<Criterion, number>;
type Scores = Record<string, Record<Criterion, number>>;

const weights: Weights = { pay: 0.25, team: 0.2, growth: 0.2, tech: 0.1, onCall: 0.1, culture: 0.15 };

const scores: Scores = {
  A: { pay: 3, team: 4, growth: 3, tech: 3, onCall: 4, culture: 4 },
  B: { pay: 4, team: 3, growth: 3, tech: 4, onCall: 2, culture: 3 },
  C: { pay: 3, team: 4, growth: 5, tech: 4, onCall: 3, culture: 3 },
};

const total = (w: Weights, s: Record<Criterion, number>) =>
  (Object.keys(w) as Criterion[]).reduce((sum, c) => sum + w[c] * s[c], 0);

const winner = (w: Weights) =>
  Object.entries(scores)
    .map(([name, s]) => ({ name, value: total(w, s) }))
    .reduce((a, b) => (b.value > a.value ? b : a));

// بنغيّر وزن واحد، ونوزّع الباقي على الأوزان التانية بنفس النسب
function reweight(w: Weights, c: Criterion, value: number): Weights {
  const rest = 1 - w[c];
  const out = { ...w };
  for (const k of Object.keys(w) as Criterion[]) out[k] = k === c ? value : (w[k] / rest) * (1 - value);
  return out;
}

export function flips(w: Weights): string[] {
  const base = winner(w).name;
  const lines: string[] = [];
  for (const c of Object.keys(w) as Criterion[]) {
    let found: string | null = null;
    // بنمشي من الوزن الحالي للطرفين خطوة 0.01، وأول تغيير في الفايز هو الحد
    for (let step = 1; step <= 100 && !found; step++) {
      for (const dir of [1, -1]) {
        const v = +(w[c] + (dir * step) / 100).toFixed(2);
        if (v < 0 || v > 1) continue;
        const next = reweight(w, c, v);
        const w2 = winner(next);
        // التعادل مش قلب: لازم عرض تاني يعدّي الفايز الحالي فعلًا
        if (w2.name !== base && w2.value > total(next, scores[base]) + 1e-9) { found = `${w[c].toFixed(2)} -> ${v.toFixed(2)} makes ${w2.name} win`; break; }
      }
    }
    lines.push(`  ${c.padEnd(9)}${found ?? "no flip in range"}`);
  }
  return lines;
}

English

Honesty is not negotiable. Never invent an offer. Besides being a lie, it's fragile: recruiters know each other, companies check, and a bluff that gets called costs you the offer you had. Haseeb's writing draws the line clearly: protecting information that might weaken your position is fine, but "I keep saying be honest and I mean it—be honest", and his last rule warns against playing games: "Don't bullshit them or play stupid games." You can decline to share details ("I'd rather not share the numbers") without saying anything untrue.

Asking for more time. Say when you'll decide and why, early, and give a reason that's true: "I'm finishing another process and I expect an answer by the 20th. Could I have until the 22nd?" Ask as soon as you know you'll need it, not on the deadline. Haseeb recommends setting your own decision deadline and stating it to every company, on a business day, so nobody's left guessing.

Declining gracefully. Thank them, say it plainly and quickly, don't explain more than you want to, and leave the door open. These people may be your colleagues or your customers in a few years. A short, warm, final "no" is a form of respect; going silent isn't.

Being honest about a competing offer doesn't mean telling every company everything. Say what is true, and say only what you are comfortable having repeated.

type Offer = { company: string; deadline: string };
type Pending = { company: string; expectedDecision: string };

const AS_OF = "2026-11-09";
const days = (from: string, to: string) => Math.round((Date.parse(to) - Date.parse(from)) / 86_400_000);

export function plan(offers: Offer[], pending: Pending[]): string[] {
  const out: string[] = [];
  const lastDecision = pending.map((p) => p.expectedDecision).sort().at(-1);
  for (const o of offers.toSorted((a, b) => a.deadline.localeCompare(b.deadline))) {
    const left = days(AS_OF, o.deadline);
    out.push(`${o.company}: deadline ${o.deadline} (${left} days left)`);
    if (lastDecision && o.deadline < lastDecision) {
      const need = days(o.deadline, lastDecision) + 2;
      // اليومين الزيادة عشان تقارن بعد آخر رد، مش تقرر في نفس اليوم
      out.push(`  ask for ${need} more days: until after ${lastDecision}`);
      out.push(`  ask today: they deserve notice, and you have ${left} days`);
    } else {
      out.push("  no extension needed");
    }
  }
  for (const p of pending.toSorted((a, b) => a.expectedDecision.localeCompare(b.expectedDecision))) {
    out.push(`${p.company}: decision expected ${p.expectedDecision}. Tell them you have a deadline.`);
  }
  return out;
}

Part 6 — The counter and the close 🔴 Advancedالجزء 6 — الـ counter والإغلاق

⏱ Timed prompt: 10 minutes You have an offer in front of you. Write a counter email with no more than three asks, each with its reason, and write the first two sentences you'd say on the phone. Then run the email through the builder.
⏱ تمرين بتوقيت: 10 دقايق. قدامك عرض. اكتب counter email بـ 3 طلبات على الأكتر، كل واحد ومعاه سببه، واكتب أول جملتين هتقولهم في التليفون. وبعدين عدّي الإيميل على الـ builder.
English

Reasons for each request. Haseeb's rule #7 is "proclaim reasons for everything": a reason turns a demand into a request someone can defend inside their company. Use only reasons that are true. A reason you can't back up is a bluff with better manners. Pick at most three asks; ranking them tells the recruiter which one to fight for.

Everything in writing. Haseeb's first rule is "Get everything in writing": from the first call, write down everything they tell you, even the things that aren't money. Then the final offer has to be written and complete before you resign from anywhere: base, bonus terms, equity and vesting, signing and clawback, start date, level, and remote arrangement. A promise made verbally on the phone doesn't exist until it's in the document.

"If you can do X, I will sign." Haseeb's closing move is only honest if it's true, so say it only when you mean it. Keep it a conversation. He also notes that calling it "discussing the details" is less adversarial than "negotiating".

If they say no. Thank them, decide calmly (you already know your floor from Part 1), and ask what could be reviewed later: a compensation review after a set period, or a non-monetary item. A "no" to one ask isn't a "no" to the offer, and a counter made respectfully should not cost you an offer. If the offer is pulled just for asking, you've learned something about that company.

A counter from your current employer. There's no formula, and no number here. Weigh why you started looking in the first place, because more money doesn't fix a bad manager or a dead-end project. Ask what changes besides pay. Notice whether it took a resignation to get a raise, and consider whether trust will change once you've said you were leaving. Promises about the future need to be in writing like any other term.

// الأداة دي بتبني الـ counter email، وبترفض أي طلب من غير سبب.
// للتشغيل: node counter-builder.ts
// الأرقام وحدات وهمية ⁦normalized (⁩أساس العرض A بيساوي 100)، مش مرتبات حقيقية.
type Ask = { item: string; from: string; to: string; reason?: string };
type Counter = { name: string; asks: Ask[]; deadlineAsk: string };

const MAX_ASKS = 3;

export function problems(c: Counter): string[] {
  const out: string[] = [];
  if (c.asks.length > MAX_ASKS) out.push(`${c.asks.length} asks: pick the ${MAX_ASKS} that matter`);
  for (const a of c.asks) if (!a.reason?.trim()) out.push(`"${a.item}" has no reason`);
  return out;
}

export function buildEmail(c: Counter): string {
  const lines = c.asks.map((a) => `  - ${a.item}: from ${a.from} to ${a.to}. ${a.reason}`);
  return [
    `Hi ${c.name},`,
    "",
    "Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about the team and the work.",
    "After reviewing the full package, I'd like to discuss these points:",
    "",
    ...lines,
    "",
    "If we can reach this, I'm ready to sign. Could you send any changes in writing,",
    `and let me know by ${c.deadlineAsk}?`,
  ].join("\n");
}

Counter من شغلك الحالي. مفيش معادلة، ومفيش أرقام هنا. فكّر ليه بدأت تدوّر أصلًا، لأن فلوس أكتر مبتصلحش manager وحش ولا مشروع مقفول. اسأل إيه اللي هيتغير غير المرتب. وخد بالك إن الزيادة جات بس بعد استقالة، وفكّر هل الثقة هتتغير بعد ما قلت إنك ماشي. وأي وعد عن المستقبل لازم يتكتب زي أي بند.

2. Find the crossover 🟡 medium Write crossover(a, b) using totalComp: the first month where the offer that was ahead falls behind. What's special about the months it finds for offers A, B and C?

Solution · الحل 
// الحل ده بيدوّر على الشهر اللي عرض بيعدّي فيه عرض تاني.
// للتشغيل: node ex-crossover.ts
// الأرقام وحدات وهمية ⁦normalized (⁩أساس العرض A بيساوي 100).
import { offers, totalComp, type Offer, type Scenario } from "./total-comp.ts";

const base: Omit<Scenario, "months"> = { equityFactor: 1, fx: 1, bonusFactor: 1 };

export function crossover(a: Offer, b: Offer, maxMonths = 60): number | null {
  // بنبدأ من الشهر 1 لأن مقارنة الشهر صفر مالهاش معنى
  let prev = totalComp(a, { ...base, months: 1 }) > totalComp(b, { ...base, months: 1 });
  for (let m = 2; m <= maxMonths; m++) {
    const now = totalComp(a, { ...base, months: m }) > totalComp(b, { ...base, months: m });
    if (now !== prev) return m;
    prev = now;
  }
  return null;
}

const [a, b, c] = offers;
const say = (x: Offer, y: Offer) => {
  const m = crossover(x, y);
  console.log(`${x.name} vs ${y.name}: ${m === null ? "no crossover in 60 months" : `crosses at month ${m}`}`);
};
say(a, b);
say(c, b);
say(c, a);

3. An honesty check 🟡 medium Write honestyCheck(message, offers), which flags a message mentioning a competing offer unless it matches an entry in your log of written offers. Run it on three messages.

Solution · الحل 
// القاعدة دي بترفض أي كلام عن عرض منافس مش موجود في سجلك.
// للتشغيل: node ex-honesty.ts
type Offer = { company: string; inWriting: boolean };

const CLAIM = /\b(offer from|competing offer|another offer)\b/i;

export function honestyCheck(message: string, offers: Offer[]): string[] {
  if (!CLAIM.test(message)) return [];
  const realCompany = offers.some((o) => o.inWriting && message.toLowerCase().includes(o.company.toLowerCase()));
  const generic = /\b(another offer|competing offer)\b/i.test(message) && offers.some((o) => o.inWriting);
  // الرسالة لازم تتكلم عن عرض مكتوب وموجود فعلًا عندك
  return realCompany || generic ? [] : ["mentions a competing offer you have not logged in writing"];
}

const myOffers: Offer[] = [{ company: "Company B", inWriting: true }];
const messages = [
  "I also have an offer from Company B, due next Friday.",
  "I also have an offer from Company Z that pays more.",
  "I'm in final rounds elsewhere and expect a decision by the 20th.",
];
for (const m of messages) console.log(JSON.stringify(honestyCheck(m, myOffers)), "<-", m);

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • Build a range from levels.fyi, Glassdoor, your network and recruiters. Check level, date and sample size. Remote and local are separate.
  • The bottom of any range you say is the figure they'll hear: it must be one you'd accept.
  • Compare the whole package over the years you expect to stay. Equity may be worth zero. Bonus target isn't a promise. Currency is a variable.
  • Defer if you can; if you can't, give a range based on data. It's a default, not a law (Haseeb: protect information, keep options open, give reasons).
  • "Current salary?": whether they may ask differs by country, so check locally, and protect the information where you can.
  • Beyond money: team, growth, tech, on-call, culture. The answers come from the questions you asked in the interviews.
  • In a weighted matrix, the useful output is what flips the decision.
  • Never invent an offer. Decline to share numbers if you like, but never say something untrue.
  • Ask for more time early, with a true reason and a date. Decline warmly and quickly.
  • Counter: at most three asks, each with a true reason. "If you can do X, I will sign" only if you mean it.
  • Get everything in writing before you resign. A verbal promise doesn't exist yet.
  • From your current employer: ask why you started looking, and what changes besides pay.
بالعامية
  • ابني range من levels.fyi و Glassdoor وشبكتك والـ recruiters، واتأكد من الـ level والتاريخ وحجم العينة، و remote منفصل عن local.
  • أقل رقم في أي range بتقوله هو اللي هيسمعوه، فلازم تكون هتقبله.
  • قارن الـ package كله على السنين اللي متوقع تقعدها. الـ equity ممكن تطلع بصفر، والـ bonus target مش وعد، والعملة متغير.
  • أجّل لو تقدر، ولو مقدرتش قول range مبني على data. ده default مش قانون (Haseeb: احمي المعلومة، خلّي اختياراتك مفتوحة، وقول أسباب).
  • "مرتبك الحالي؟": إن كان مسموح يسألوا بيختلف من بلد لبلد، فارجع للقواعد عندك، واحمي المعلومة لو تقدر.
  • ما بعد الفلوس: الفريق، الـ growth، الـ tech، الـ on-call، الـ culture، والإجابات جاية من الأسئلة اللي سألتها في الـ interviews.
  • في المصفوفة الموزونة، الناتج المفيد هو اللي بيقلب القرار.
  • عمرك ما تألف عرض. ممكن ترفض تقول الأرقام، بس عمرك ما تقول حاجة مش حقيقية.

  • الـ counter: 3 طلبات على الأكتر، وكل واحد بسبب حقيقي. و"لو عملتوا X، همضي" بس لو تقصدها.

  • من شغلك الحالي: اسأل نفسك بدأت تدوّر ليه، وإيه اللي هيتغير غير المرتب.
