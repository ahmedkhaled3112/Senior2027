Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

Your code lives inside your team, but your deadlines often don't. A common pattern: the feature is finished and then waits on a Redis cluster from infra, an event from the notifications team, or a table from data. How that wait goes depends less on the ticket than on three things: whether those people know you, whether you understand what's squeezing them, and whether the deal is written down. In interviews it comes up as "tell me about a conflict with another team."

Treat this lesson as advice. The Orders team, the names and every number in the scenarios are invented for teaching, and the mid-versus-senior contrasts are teaching patterns, not measurements. The team sits at a mid-size online store: Mona (product manager), Hany (engineering manager), Karim (mid-level), Salma (junior), Nour (infra), Yasmin (support lead), and you. Also appearing here: Rana (Data lead), Omar (Notifications lead), Tarek (Catalog lead) and Laila (Catalog's manager).

بالعامية

الكود بتاعك جوه فريقك، بس الـ deadlines في الغالب لأ. سيناريو متكرر: الـ feature خلصت وقاعدة مستنية Redis cluster من الـ infra، أو event من فريق الـ notifications، أو table من الـ data. والانتظار ده هيمشي إزاي مش بيتوقف على الـ ticket قد ما بيتوقف على 3 حاجات: الناس دي تعرفك أصلًا ولا لأ، إنت فاهم إيه اللي ضاغطهم ولا لأ، والاتفاق مكتوب ولا لأ. وفي الـ interviews بييجي في صورة "احكيلي عن conflict حصل مع فريق تاني".

خد الدرس ده كنصايح. فريق الـ Orders والأسامي وكل الأرقام في السيناريوهات متألفة للتعليم، والمقارنات بين الـ Mid والـ Senior أنماط للشرح مش حاجة متقاسة. الفريق في online store متوسط الحجم: منى (product manager)، هاني (engineering manager)، كريم (mid-level)، سلمى (junior)، نور (infra)، ياسمين (support lead)، وإنت. وهيظهر كمان في الدرس: رنا (lead فريق الـ Data)، عمر (lead فريق الـ Notifications)، طارق (lead فريق الـ Catalog)، وليلى (manager فريق الـ Catalog).

Part 1 — Before you need them 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 1 — قبل ما تحتاجهم

English

The situation. Mona wants a daily refunds report in the admin by launch week, and it needs a refunds_daily table from the data team. You've never spoken to anyone on that team. Your first message to Rana, their lead, is a ticket link and "we need this by Thursday."

What a mid-level engineer does. Makes contact only when blocked. The other team's first impression is a demand with a deadline they had no part in setting, and it lands on top of whatever they've already promised someone else.

What a senior does. Has the conversation months earlier: 20 minutes, no request, real questions. What's on your plate? What do other teams do that makes your life harder? Then they do one small thing with the answer, such as warning the data team before a column rename, so the next contact starts from "you're the one who sorted that out."

Why the second works. You learn their constraints while nobody is under pressure, and you pay first: they get something before you ask for anything. It also gives you something to bring to decisions. Will Larson lists "context from a critical team" among the things that earn a seat where decisions get made (Getting in the room). The same conversation works with product, design, data, infra and support.

To: Rana (Data). Direct message.

Hi Rana. I work on the Orders backend. We've never really worked together, and I'd like to fix that before either of us needs something from the other.

Could I take 20 minutes next week, whenever suits you? Nothing to prepare, and there's no request hiding behind this.

Three things I'd like to ask:

1. What's on your plate this quarter, and what's eating most of it?
2. What do other teams do, Orders included, that makes your life harder? I suspect our column renames on the orders table hurt your nightly load, but I don't actually know.
3. Is there anything we could publish or warn you about that would save you time?

If this is a bad month, just say so and I'll ask again next quarter.

الموقف. منى عايزة report يومي للـ refunds في الـ admin قبل أسبوع الإطلاق، وده محتاج جدول refunds_daily من فريق الـ data. إنت عمرك ما كلمت حد في الفريق ده. وأول رسالة منك لرنا، الـ lead بتاعتهم، هي لينك ticket و"محتاجين ده يوم الخميس".

الـ Mid بيعمل إيه. مبيكلمش حد غير لما يتعطل. فأول انطباع عند الفريق التاني عنك هو طلب بـ deadline هما ماشاركوش في تحديده، وبييجي فوق اللي وعدوا بيه ناس تانية أصلًا.

ليه التاني بينجح. بتعرف الـ constraints بتاعتهم وماحدش تحت ضغط، وإنت اللي بتدفع الأول: هما بياخدوا حاجة قبل ما تطلب أي حاجة. وكمان بيبقى معاك حاجة تفيد في القرارات: Will Larson بيعدّ "context from a critical team" (يعني سياق من فريق مهم) من الحاجات اللي بتجيب لك مكان في الأوضة اللي القرارات بتتاخد فيها. ونفس القعدة تنفع مع الـ product والـ design والـ data والـ infra والـ support.

الرسالة زي ما هتتكتب عندنا:

"صباح الخير يا رنا. أنا شغال على الـ backend بتاع الـ Orders. إحنا عمرنا ما اشتغلنا مع بعض بجد، وعايز أغيّر ده قبل ما حد فينا يحتاج حاجة من التاني. ينفع آخد منك 20 دقيقة الأسبوع الجاي في أي وقت يناسبك؟ مش محتاج تحضير، ومفيش طلب مستخبي ورا الموضوع.

عايز أسألك 3 حاجات:

  1. إيه اللي عليكم الربع ده، وإيه أكتر حاجة واكلة وقتكم؟
  2. إيه الحاجات اللي الفرق التانية بتعملها، وإحنا منهم، وبتصعّب شغلكم؟ أنا شاكك إن الـ column renames بتاعتنا على جدول الـ orders بتبوّظ الـ nightly load عندكم، بس مش متأكد.

Trade-off: time now, no visible return These chats take time and pay nothing this sprint, and if you skip them nobody notices until the crunch. So keep them small and regular. Part 2 — Their constraints 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 2 — القيود بتاعتهم

English

The situation. The invoice-pdf and notifications queues share a Redis instance with the product cache. BullMQ needs maxmemory-policy noeviction and the cache wants eviction, and that setting is per instance (BullMQ core shows what goes wrong), so you ask Nour for a dedicated cluster. The answer: not for a month. Karim writes "infra is blocking us again" in the team channel.

What a mid-level engineer does. Complains upward. Hany now has a conflict and no facts, Nour hears about it from somebody else, and nobody has learned why the answer was no.

What a senior does. Asks first, and finds out there's a production change freeze until March 1 and a budget review on February 24. Then asks the two questions that turn a no into a plan: "What would need to be true for this to work in March? And what can we do on the existing cluster until then?"

Why the second works. A no from another team is usually protecting something, and you can't design around what you haven't heard. Sometimes the no really is wrong, but even then you need the reason before you can say so. Once you know it, the constraint is an input to your plan, like a rate limit. Team Topologies gives you words for this: it names three ways teams should interact, which are Collaboration (working together for a defined period), X-as-a-Service (one provides, one consumes) and Facilitation (one helps and mentors the other). A one-off cluster isn't X-as-a-Service, whatever the ticket form suggests. It's a short collaboration and needs planning like one, with Nour's time actually booked.

--- 1. You to Nour, direct message ---

Hi Nour. I saw your reply about the dedicated Redis. Before I take this anywhere, I'd rather understand what's going on at your end than guess.

Why we asked: invoice-pdf and notifications share the Redis that holds the product cache. BullMQ needs noeviction and the cache wants eviction, and that's one setting per instance. Memory reached 81% during last Tuesday's evening peak. Nothing broke, and I'd like it to stay that way.

What's making this hard right now: time, budget, a freeze, something I haven't thought of?

Once I understand that, two questions:
1. What would need to be true for this to work in March?
2. What can we do on the existing cluster until then that doesn't add work for you?

--- 2. Nour's reply, the next morning ---

Prod infra is frozen until March 1 for the network move. A new cluster is unplanned spend, about EGP 31,000 a month, so it has to go through the budget review on Feb 24. If Hany puts one paragraph in that review, I can build it in March, as soon as I have a free week. Until then, no config changes from me, but anything you do in the app is fine.

--- 3. You, in the team channel ---

Update on the dedicated Redis: we're not blocked, we're sequenced.

- Why not now: infra change freeze until March 1, and the spend needs the Feb 24 budget review.
- What we need: one paragraph from Hany for the review. I've drafted it (link).
- Until then, app side only: removeOnComplete with a count limit on invoice-pdf, and the job carries the order id instead of the whole order. PR today.
- If memory passes 90% before March: I call Nour and we ask for a freeze exception together.

Owner: me. Next update: Feb 25, after the review.

الموقف. الـ queues بتوع invoice-pdf وnotifications شغالين على نفس الـ Redis اللي فيه الـ product cache. BullMQ محتاج maxmemory-policy noeviction والـ cache عايز eviction، والـ setting دي واحدة للـ instance كله (درس BullMQ بيوريك إيه اللي بيحصل لو اتلخبطت). فطلبت من نور cluster منفصل، والرد: مش قبل شهر. كريم كتب في channel الفريق "الـ infra معطلينا تاني".

الـ Mid بيعمل إيه. بيشتكي لفوق. هاني دلوقتي بقى عنده conflict من غير أي facts، ونور بيسمع الكلام من حد تاني، ومحدش عرف أصلًا ليه الرد كان لأ.

ليه التاني بينجح. الـ "لأ" من فريق تاني غالبًا بتحمي حاجة، ومش هتعرف تصمّم حوالين حاجة ماسمعتهاش. وساعات الـ "لأ" بتبقى غلط فعلًا، بس حتى ساعتها لازم تعرف السبب عشان تقدر تقول كده. ولما تعرفه، الـ constraint بيبقى input للخطة بتاعتك، زي الـ rate limit بالظبط. وTeam Topologies بيديك كلام تسمّي بيه ده: 3 طرق بس الفرق المفروض تتعامل بيها: Collaboration (شغل مع بعض لفترة محددة)، X-as-a-Service (حد بيقدّم وحد بيستهلك)، وFacilitation (فريق بيساعد ويعلّم فريق تاني). والـ cluster اللي بيتعمل مرة واحدة ده مش X-as-a-Service مهما كان شكل الـ ticket. ده collaboration قصير ولازم يتخطط كده، ووقت نور يتحجز فعلًا.

الكلام زي ما هيتقال عندنا. الرسالة لنور:

"صباح الخير يا نور. شفت ردك بخصوص الـ Redis المنفصل. قبل ما أحرّك الموضوع مع أي حد، عايز أفهم إيه اللي عندك بدل ما أخمّن. طلبناه ليه: الـ invoice-pdf والـ notifications على نفس الـ Redis اللي فيه الـ product cache، وBullMQ محتاج noeviction والـ cache عايز eviction، ودي setting واحدة للـ instance. الـ memory وصلت 81% في الـ peak بتاع بالليل يوم التلات اللي فات. محصلش حاجة، وعايزها تفضل كده. إيه اللي مصعّب الموضوع دلوقتي؟ وقت، budget، freeze، ولا حاجة تانية مش واخد بالي منها؟ ولما أفهم ده عندي سؤالين: إيه اللي لازم يبقى صح عشان يمشي في مارس؟ وإيه اللي نقدر نعمله على الـ cluster الحالي من غير ما نزوّد عليك شغل؟"

Interview question: "How do you get another team to prioritise your request?" A strong answer, as someone on the Orders team would give it: "I start by finding out what they're already committed to, not by arguing that my thing matters more. When we asked infra for a dedicated Redis, the answer was not for a month. I asked why, and learned about a freeze and a budget review. So we made it cheap to say yes: our manager wrote one paragraph for the review, and we made app-side changes that needed nothing from infra. It went through the review and got built as soon as infra had a free week." The interviewer is listening for whether you understood their priorities and what you gave up.

Part 3 — Interface agreements in writing 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 3 — اتفاقات الـ interfaces مكتوبة

English

The situation. Customers should get one SMS and one email when a refund completes. In a corridor chat you say "we'll publish the event and you retry, right?" and Omar says "I thought you'd retry until we confirm." Nothing is built yet, and each team has been assuming the other handles retries.

What a mid-level engineer does. Leaves with a verbal "sounds good" and starts building. The gap shows up in production: when the SMS provider has a bad hour, neither side retries and customers get nothing, or both do and customers get three messages.

What a senior does. Writes one page before anyone builds and asks the other team to edit it, not just approve it. It covers event names and fields, who owns retries, idempotency, expected volume, how changes get announced, and who to call.

Why the second works. A page forces the questions a friendly chat skips. Disagreement is cheap in a doc comment and expensive at 2 a.m. And the page outlives the two people who agreed. The technical side (versioning, tolerant readers, contract tests) is in APIs as Contracts and REST API Design. This part is the negotiation: who gives up what, and who's on the hook.

# Interface agreement: refund notifications

Between: Orders (publishes), Notifications (consumes)
Editors: you (Orders), Omar (Notifications)
Status: AGREED on Feb 12, nobody objected by the deadline

## What this covers
When a refund completes, the customer gets one SMS and one email.

## The event
Name: `refund.completed`, version 1, on the `notifications` queue.

- eventId (UUID): unique per refund, the same across retries
- refundId, orderId, userId (UUID)
- amountMinor (integer): piastres, so 12550 means EGP 125.50
- currency: "EGP" only, for now
- completedAt: ISO 8601, UTC
- No phone number or email in the event. Notifications looks them up by userId.

## Who owns what
- Publishing: Orders. Written in the same transaction as the refund (outbox).
- Getting it onto the queue: Orders. The relay retries until the queue accepts it.
- Sending the SMS and email: Notifications. 5 tries with backoff over about 2 hours.
- After the last failure: Notifications. Job stays in the failed set, alert goes to #notif-oncall.
- Duplicates: Notifications. Dedupe on eventId for 7 days. Orders may publish the same event twice.
- Wrong amount, missing refund: Orders is the source of truth.

## Expected volume
Normal: about 400 refunds a day.
Peak, after a big sale: up to 3,000 a day, at most 30 a minute.
Orders gives one week's notice before anything that could exceed this, such as a bulk refund script.

## Changes

The situation. A customer gets two invoices for one order. # 15-minute demo: why our BullMQ jobs run twice

Audience: anyone running a BullMQ worker (Orders, Notifications, Catalog)
When: Thursday 1:00 pm, small meeting room and the call link
Afterwards: the write-up lives at wiki/queues/bullmq-stalled-jobs

0:00  The symptom (2 min). Two invoice PDFs for one order. Show the two log lines: same jobId, 31 seconds apart.
0:02  What a stall is (3 min). The worker holds a lock and renews it. If the event loop is blocked, the renewal doesn't run, the lock expires, and another worker gets the job.
0:05  Live demo (5 min). Worker with a CPU-heavy PDF render. Watch the stalled event fire. Same job in a sandboxed processor: no stall.
0:10  What to check in your queue (3 min). See the checklist.
0:13  Questions (2 min).

Checklist:
- Is any handler CPU-heavy (PDF, image resize)? Run it in a sandboxed processor.
- Do you alert on the stalled event? Without one, a stall looks like a healthy worker.
- Is the handler idempotent? Even after the fix, a crash can run a job twice.

English

The situation. Nour has one free week before April. Orders needs it for the dedicated Redis. Tarek's Catalog team needs it to move the search index. Both teams have told people a date.

What a mid-level engineer does. Escalates to Hany straight away, or fights Tarek in a thread where forty people can watch. Either way the managers inherit a fight with two different sets of facts.

What a senior does. Talks to Tarek first with shared facts, agrees with Nour what's actually possible, proposes a joint recommendation, and escalates together only if they can't agree. The words: "We can't both have that week. Here's what each of us loses, and what we'd suggest."

Here the recommendation goes against your own team, and that's what makes it credible: Catalog's risk has a date and no workaround, and yours has a workaround.

To: Hany (Orders), Laila (Catalog)
Cc: Nour
From: you and Tarek
Subject: Nour's free week: we need a decision by Thursday

We can't both have that week. Here's what each of us loses, and what we'd suggest.

The facts we agree on (Nour has checked these)
- Nour has one free week before April: the week of March 7.
- Orders needs it for the dedicated Redis, about 4 days.
- Catalog needs it for the search index move, about 5 days.
- Neither job can be split or handed to someone else.

If Catalog gets the week, Orders loses
- Queues keep sharing Redis with the cache for 3 more weeks.
- Risk: memory hit 81% at peak before the app-side changes, about 70% since.
- Workaround: yes. If memory passes 90%, we can turn off the coupon cache.

If Orders gets the week, Catalog loses
- The index move slips to the first week of April.
- Risk: at today's growth the current index runs out of disk around March 20.
- Workaround: none that Nour or Tarek trust.

What we suggest
Catalog takes the week of March 7. Orders takes the first week of April. If Redis memory passes 90% before then, Orders gets priority and Nour switches to it.

What we need from you
A yes, or a different call, by Thursday so Nour can plan. Whichever you choose, we'll both go with it.

Their risk had a date and no workaround, and ours had a workaround, so I recommended they go first, and we sent one note to both managers. It was decided within a day." The interviewer is listening for talking before escalating, a fair account of the other side, and no villain. For the answer's shape, see section 16 · 1.4 and 2.3 (behavioral answers in STAR form).
سؤال interview: "احكيلي عن conflict مع فريق تاني." إجابة قوية عن فريق الـ Orders: "إحنا وCatalog كنا محتاجين الـ infra engineer الوحيد في نفس الأسبوع، والفريقين كانوا واعدين بمواعيد. بدل ما أصعّد، رحت للـ lead بتاع Catalog. خلينا الـ infra engineer يأكد الـ facts، وبعدين كتبنا كل فريق هيخسر إيه. الـ risk بتاعهم كان ليه ميعاد ومفيش workaround، وبتاعنا ليه workaround، فاقترحت إنهم يبدأوا الأول، وبعتنا نوت واحدة للـ managers الاتنين. القرار اتاخد في يوم." اللي بيقيّمك بيسمع: كلمت قبل ما تصعّد؟ حكيت عن الطرف التاني بإنصاف؟ مفيش شرير في الحكاية؟ وشكل الإجابة موجود في قسم 16 · 1.4 و 2.3 (إجابات الـ behavioral بطريقة STAR).

Common mistakes · غلطات شائعة

❌ First contact as a demand 
@Rana we need the `refunds_daily` table by Thursday for the launch. Ticket: DATA-418. Please prioritize, Mona is asking.
✅ First contact as a conversation 
Hi Rana. Orders needs a `refunds_daily` table for the admin. Launch is Thursday, so I'm late telling you, and that's on us.

What's realistic on your side? If it's not this week, a CSV by hand on Thursday gets us through launch and the table can follow.

Fields are in DATA-418.
بالعامية

❌ الرسالة الأولى طلب بـ deadline واسم منى للضغط، ورنا ماتعرفش عنك أي حاجة. هتتحط في الطابور. ✅ التانية بتعترف إن التأخير عندك، بتسأل إيه الواقعي، وبتدي مخرج سهل (CSV باليد يوم الخميس والجدول بعدين). نفس الطلب، بس بقى كلام بين اتنين بيحاولوا يحلوا مشكلة. "إحنا اتأخرنا نقولك، والغلطة عندنا. إيه الممكن عندكم؟"

❌ "They're blocking us" 
Posted in #orders-eng:
Infra is blocking us again. Nour says a month for the Redis cluster. @Hany can you push on this?
✅ Asking about the constraint 
To Nour:
Saw "not for a month" on the Redis cluster. What's making it hard right now: time, budget, a freeze? And what would need to be true for it to work in March?
بالعامية

❌ الأولى بتحكم على الفريق قبل ما تعرف حاجة، وبتحط هاني في النص من غير facts، ونور هيقرا الكلام ده من غير ما حد يكون سأله أصلًا. ✅ التانية بتروح للشخص نفسه وبتسأل السبب، وبتحوّل الـ "لأ" لشروط. "إيه اللي مصعّب ده دلوقتي، وإيه اللي لازم يبقى صح عشان يمشي في مارس؟" ولو بعد ما عرفت السبب لسه شايف إن فيه مشكلة، ساعتها روح لهاني ومعاك facts.

❌ Verbal assumption 
Chatted with Omar, we're aligned on refund notifications. He'll handle it on his side.
✅ Written agreement 
Omar and I drafted one page on refund notifications (link).
Retries: Notifications. Duplicates: Notifications, dedupe on eventId for 7 days. Publishing: Orders, through the outbox.
Omar's edits are in. Objections by Thursday, or it stands.
بالعامية

❌ "متفقين" من غير أي تفاصيل معناها إن كل واحد ماشي بفهمه هو. "هيتعامل معاها" بتغطي على مين بيعمل retry ومين بيمنع التكرار. ✅ التانية بتحط القرارات اللي ممكن يختلفوا عليها في سطور قليلة: الـ retries عند Notifications، والتكرار عند Notifications بـ dedupe على الـ eventId لمدة 7 أيام، والنشر عند Orders عن طريق الـ outbox، وتعديلات عمر موجودة، و"أي اعتراض لحد الخميس وإلا ماشيين".

Try it yourself · جرّب بنفسك

1. Book one chat 🟢 easy This week, book a 20-minute chat with someone from another team and ask the three questions from Part 1. Write the invitation by hand, not with AI. Afterwards, do one small thing with what you heard and tell them you did it. If there's no other team at your job, talk to a maintainer or community member of a tool you use.
الأسبوع ده احجز قعدة 20 دقيقة مع حد من فريق تاني، واسأله الـ 3 أسئلة بتوع الجزء الأول. اكتب الدعوة بإيدك مش بالـ AI. وبعدها اعمل حاجة صغيرة باللي سمعته وقوله إنك عملتها. ولو مفيش فريق تاني في شغلك، كلم maintainer أو حد من community أداة بتستخدمها.
Hint · تلميح Pick the team that blocked you most recently, or the team your changes break without you noticing.
اختار الفريق اللي عطّلك آخر مرة، أو الفريق اللي تعديلاتك بتبوّظله حاجات من غير ما تاخد بالك.
Solution · الحل Worked example from the Orders team: you pick Yasmin. Her answer to question 2 is that refunds show "Processing" for three days in the admin, so agents keep messaging engineers. You add a "provider accepted on" line to the admin page, half a day of work, and tell her when it ships. Two months later, when a cutover needs a customer notice, she offers to write it.
مثال من فريق الـ Orders: اخترت ياسمين. إجابتها على السؤال التاني إن الـ refunds بتفضل "Processing" 3 أيام في الـ admin، فالـ agents بيفضلوا يبعتوا للـ engineers. ضفت سطر "الـ provider قبله يوم كذا" في صفحة الـ admin، نص يوم شغل، وقلتلها لما نزل. وبعد شهرين، لما cutover احتاج رسالة للعملاء، هي اللي عرضت تكتبها.
2. One page, edited by them 🟡 medium Pick a live dependency between your team and another, and write a one-page interface agreement with the headings from Part 3, by hand and not with AI. Then ask the other side to edit it, not just approve it. If your job has no such dependency, write it for a third-party API your side project in project/ calls, and ask a friend to play the other team.
اختار dependency شغالة بين فريقك وفريق تاني، واكتب اتفاق interface في صفحة واحدة بنفس عناوين الجزء التالت، بإيدك مش بالـ AI. وبعدين اطلب من الطرف التاني يعدّل فيها مش يوافق وخلاص. ولو مفيش dependency زي دي في شغلك، اكتبها لـ third-party API بيستخدمها المشروع الجانبي في project/، وخلي صاحبك يلعب دور الفريق التاني.
Hint · تلميح Start with the question you're least sure about. It's usually "who retries?" or "who hears about a change first?"
ابدأ بالسؤال اللي إنت مش متأكد من إجابته. غالبًا "مين بيعيد؟" أو "مين بيعرف بالتغيير الأول؟"
Solution · الحل Worked example: Karim writes one for the nightly orders export to Data. His draft said "we'll tell you about changes." Rana's edits: the export can land as late as 04:00 rather than 02:00, coupon_code stays nullable, and column renames need two weeks' notice. Those three edits are exactly what a verbal agreement would have missed.
مثال: كريم كتب واحدة للـ nightly export بتاع الـ orders لفريق الـ Data. المسودة كان فيها "هنقولكم على التغييرات". تعديلات رنا: الـ export ممكن يوصل لحد 04:00 مش 02:00، وcoupon_code يفضل nullable، وأي column rename محتاج إنذار قبلها بأسبوعين. الـ 3 تعديلات دول بالظبط اللي الاتفاق الشفوي كان هيفوّته.
3. Teach one thing you alone know 🔴 at work Give a 15-minute internal demo, or publish one doc on your internal wiki, about something only you know. Use the outline from Part 4 and prepare it by hand, not with AI. Keep company code and customer data inside the company. If you have no internal audience, record a demo of your side project in project/ instead.
اعمل demo داخلي 15 دقيقة، أو انشر doc واحد على الـ wiki الداخلي، عن حاجة إنت بس اللي عارفها. استخدم الـ outline بتاع الجزء الرابع وحضّرها بإيدك مش بالـ AI. وخلي كود الشركة وبيانات العملاء جوه الشركة. ولو مفيش جمهور داخلي، سجّل demo للمشروع الجانبي في project/ بدلها.
Hint · تلميح Look at what people keep asking you in direct messages. That's the topic.
بص على الحاجات اللي الناس بتفضل تسألك عليها في الـ DMs. ده الموضوع.
Solution · الحل Worked example: Salma gives "How to read a payment-webhook failure in the logs": one real failed event with the customer details removed, three log lines to look for, one dashboard link. After it, Yasmin's team stops forwarding those tickets without the event id.
مثال: سلمى عملت "إزاي تقرا فشل payment-webhook في الـ logs": event واحد فاشل بجد بعد ما شالت بيانات العميل، و3 سطور logs تدور عليهم، ولينك dashboard واحد. وبعدها فريق ياسمين بطّل يحوّل التذاكر دي من غير الـ event id.

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • Talk before you need anything: 20 minutes, real questions, no request hiding behind it.
  • Do one small thing with what you heard. That's what makes the next conversation different.
  • Small and regular: about one chat a month, rotating through product, design, data, infra and support.
  • A no is usually protecting something. Find out what before you tell anyone you're blocked.
  • The two questions: "What would need to be true for this to work in March?" and "What can we do until then?"
  • Three ways teams should interact: Collaboration, X-as-a-Service, Facilitation. Know which one you're asking for.
  • One page before anyone builds: event and fields, retries owner, idempotency, volume, change notice, who to call.
  • Write down what each side gave up. That's the negotiation, and it stops it being reopened later.
  • Same bug in another team? A 15-minute demo, a short doc, and credit to whoever found it first.
  • Teams collide: the other lead first, facts confirmed by the owner, one joint note.
  • The words: "We can't both have that week. Here's what each of us loses, and what we'd suggest."
  • Customer being hurt right now? Skip the ladder. Page someone and explain later.
بالعامية
  • اتكلم قبل ما تحتاج أي حاجة: 20 دقيقة، أسئلة حقيقية، ومفيش طلب مستخبي.
  • اعمل حاجة صغيرة باللي سمعته. ده اللي بيغيّر الكلام المرة الجاية.
  • صغيرة ومنتظمة: حوالي قعدة في الشهر، بالدور على الـ product والـ design والـ data والـ infra والـ support.
  • الـ "لأ" غالبًا بتحمي حاجة. اعرف إيه هي قبل ما تقول لحد إنك متعطل.
  • السؤالين: "إيه اللي لازم يبقى صح عشان يمشي في مارس؟" و"إيه اللي نقدر نعمله لحد ساعتها؟"
  • 3 طرق تتعامل بيها الفرق: Collaboration، وX-as-a-Service، وFacilitation. اعرف إنت طالب أنهي واحدة.
  • صفحة واحدة قبل ما حد يبني: الـ event والـ fields، مين ماسك الـ retries، الـ idempotency، الـ volume، إنذار التغيير، ونكلم مين.
  • اكتب كل طرف اتنازل عن إيه. ده التفاوض نفسه، وده اللي بيمنع إن الموضوع يتفتح تاني.
  • نفس الـ bug عند فريق تاني؟ demo 15 دقيقة، doc قصير، والـ credit للي لقاها الأول.
  • الفرق اتصادمت: الـ lead التاني الأول، الـ facts يأكدها صاحبها، ونوت واحدة مشتركة.
  • الكلام: "إحنا الاتنين مش هينفع ناخد الأسبوع ده. ده اللي كل واحد هيخسره، وده اللي بنقترحه."
  • فيه عميل بيتأذي دلوقتي؟ سيبك من السلم. كلم حد حالًا والشرح بعدين.
