Why this matters · ليه ده مهم

English

Mentoring is the part of senior work that keeps paying after you log off: every question a junior can answer alone is one nobody has to answer at 23:00. It's also easy to fake, because being the person who always knows feels like mentoring. A fix pasted into chat solves today's problem and books next month's question. This lesson is the harder habit: getting someone to the answer, giving feedback they can use, handing them work that stretches them, and keeping written evidence that they got better. A common pattern in behavioral interviews for senior roles is "tell me about someone you helped grow", and this lesson is how you'd have a true answer.

The whole section is one story: the Orders team at a mid-size online store. Mona is the product manager, Hany the engineering manager, Karim a mid-level engineer, Salma a junior engineer, Nour the infra engineer and Yasmin the support lead. "You" is you. The team, names and numbers in the scenarios are invented for teaching, and the Mid-versus-Senior contrasts are teaching patterns, not measurements.

Part 1 — Guide, don't answer 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 1 — وجّه، متديش الإجابة

English

The situation. Monday morning, 9 March. Salma sends you a direct message: "Why does this query take 8 seconds?" It's the notification history tab in the Next.js admin, and Yasmin's support team opens that tab for every complaint.

What a mid-level engineer does. Reads the query and pastes the CREATE INDEX into the chat. Ninety seconds, the page is fast, everybody's happy. Salma has gained one index. The method that found it stayed in your head, so next month she'll be back with a different slow query.

What a senior does. Climbs a ladder one rung at a time and stops as soon as she's moving: what have you tried, what does EXPLAIN show, which node takes the time, then a hint that points at a place without giving the answer, then show it and narrate how you found it, and last do it together on a call.

Why the second works. Every rung leaves the next step with her, so she practises the diagnosis and not just the fix. Julia Evans's How to answer questions in a helpful way is the best short guide to the small moves: find out what the person already knows, ask what prompted the question, link to the specific part of the docs and check it actually helped, and explain how you found the answer, including how you ruled things out. Her rule from the Recurse Center matters most at rung two: don't act surprised. If Salma asks what EXPLAIN is, "you don't know EXPLAIN?" ends this conversation and the next one. How to read the plan itself is in the EXPLAIN lesson.

The exceptions. If production's on fire, give the fix now and teach afterwards. If it's a plain fact, like the staging URL, just answer: turning every question into a quiz is its own kind of rude. And when the same question comes up again and again, Evans's advice is to write the doc and point people at it.

Part 2 — Pairing as a habit 🟡 Intermediateالجزء 2 — الـ pairing كعادة

English

The situation. On the Orders team, pairing means a panicked call at 17:00 when something's stuck. The more experienced person takes the keyboard, it gets fixed, and the other one watched.

What a mid-level engineer does. Pairs only when someone's stuck, drives because it's faster, and ends the call with "OK, it works, merge it". Nothing written down, nothing repeated.

What a senior does. Puts one hour a week on the calendar with one person. The less experienced person types. It ends with a 3-line note on what was learned. Martin Fowler's site has the practical guide, On Pair Programming by Birgitta Böckeler and Nina Siessegger. It names the styles: driver and navigator, ping-pong (one writes a failing test, the other makes it pass), and strong-style, where an idea has to reach the computer through the other person's hands. Strong-style suits knowledge transfer, and the article warns that it borders on micro-management and shouldn't be overused. Its small rules are worth stealing: as navigator wait five seconds before pointing out a mistake, watch for keyboard hogging, and as the more experienced person stay in the navigator's seat most of the time.

Why the second works. A recurring slot survives the sprint's urgency, and it's the place she'll ask what she'd never ask in a PR comment. Typing forces her through the details, and writing the note forces her to recall them. The article also names the risk: a junior who always pairs can start to wonder "could I really do this by myself?", so solo work with a check-in has to stay in the mix. That's Part 5.

# Pairing hour: Tue 17 Mar 2026, 14:00-15:00 (recurring, Tuesdays)
Driver: Salma. Navigator: You.
Style: strong-style for the first 20 minutes, then driver/navigator.

## First, the leftover from last week (5 min)
Why no Sort without DESC in the index? Postgres can read a B-tree backwards.

## Goal (one sentence)
Add a retry policy to the `notifications` queue so a provider timeout stops losing messages.

## Done means
- [x] Job options: attempts 5, exponential backoff starting at 2 s
- [x] A permanent failure (invalid phone number) does not retry
- [x] A test proves a job that fails twice succeeds on the third attempt
- [ ] PR opened by Salma (finished on her own on Wednesday)

## Plan
00-05  Salma says the goal back in her own words
05-25  Salma types the job options. I ask, I don't dictate
25-30  A real break, away from the screen
30-50  What if this job runs twice? What if the provider is down for an hour?
50-60  Stop typing. Salma writes the note below

## Note (3 lines, written by Salma at the end)
1. Learned: attempts counts the first run, so 5 means 4 retries.
2. Still fuzzy: what the worker does with a job when we deploy.
3. Next week: read the worker shutdown section together.

الـ Mid بيعمل إيه. بيعمل pairing بس لما حد يبقى stuck، وبيسوق هو عشان أسرع، وبيقفل الـ call بـ "تمام، شغالة، اعمل merge". مفيش حاجة اتكتبت، ومفيش حاجة هتتكرر.

السنيور بيعمل إيه. بيحط ساعة في الأسبوع على الـ calendar مع شخص واحد. الأقل خبرة هو اللي بيكتب. والجلسة بتنتهي بملاحظة من 3 سطور عن اللي اتعلمناه. على موقع Martin Fowler فيه الدليل العملي: مقال On Pair Programming بتاع Birgitta Böckeler وNina Siessegger. المقال بيسمّي الأساليب: driver وnavigator، وping-pong (واحد يكتب test فاشل والتاني يخليه ينجح)، وstrong-style، اللي فيه الفكرة لازم توصل للكمبيوتر عن طريق إيد الشخص التاني. الـ strong-style مناسب لنقل المعرفة، والمقال بيحذّر إنه قريب من الـ micro-management ومينفعش يتستخدم كتير. وقواعده الصغيرة تستاهل تتاخد: وإنت navigator استنى 5 ثواني قبل ما تقول على غلطة، خد بالك لو بتستولي على الكيبورد، ولو إنت الأكتر خبرة خليك في مكان الـ navigator أغلب الوقت.

ليه ده بينجح. الميعاد الثابت بيعيش رغم استعجال الـ sprint، وهو المكان اللي هتسأل فيه اللي عمرها ما هتسأله في comment على PR. والكتابة بإيدها بتعدّيها على التفاصيل، وكتابة الملاحظة بتخليها تفتكرها. والمقال كمان بيسمّي الخطر: الجونيور اللي دايمًا بيعمل pairing ممكن يبدأ يسأل نفسه "هل أقدر أعمل ده لوحدي فعلًا؟"، فلازم يفضل فيه شغل لوحده مع check-in. وده الجزء 5.

Trade-off Pairing is tiring, and the article says so: schedule breaks, and don't try to do it all day. That's why this is one hour with a real break in it. It's also a poor fit for some tasks. Well-worn boilerplate may not need two people, though the article notes that rote work can be a smell of a missing abstraction. When neither of you knows the territory, split up, read, and come back.

Why the second works, and what the evidence is. There's early evidence for the first two rows of the rule below. In January 2026 Anthropic, an AI company studying its own category of product, published a randomized trial with 52 mostly junior engineers learning the Python async library Trio. On a quiz right after the task the AI group averaged about 50% and the hand-coding group about 67%. The AI group finished about two minutes faster, which wasn't statistically significant, and the widest gap was on debugging questions. People who used AI to ask for explanations did better than those who delegated, though the authors don't claim that part is causal. The limits are real. The authors call the sample small and the result preliminary, the quiz only measured understanding shortly afterwards, and the setup was a sidebar assistant rather than an agent like Salma's (the authors expect the effect may be stronger with agents, but didn't measure it). It doesn't prove long-term harm. Use it as a reason to be deliberate, not as proof.

مثال من الفريق المتألف: كريم سأل ليه job في BullMQ اتنفذت مرتين. الدرجة 1: "جرّبت إيه؟" بص في الـ logs ولقى سطرين completed. الدرجة 2: "التشغيلتين ليهم نفس الـ job id؟" طلعوا مختلفين. يقف هنا: id مختلف معناها إن الـ job اتضافت مرتين، يعني الـ bug في الـ producer. ملاحظتك: "وقفت عند الدرجة 2. لقاها بنفسه في 6 دقايق. وأنا كنت هخمّن stalled jobs وكنت هطلع غلطان".
2. Give one specific positive SBI 🟡 medium This week, by hand, give one colleague one piece of specific positive feedback in SBI form: the situation with a date or PR, what they did with no adjectives, and what it changed. If you have no teammate to give it to, say it to a friend about something they built, or thank a maintainer on a merged open-source PR the same way.
الأسبوع ده، بإيدك، قول لزميل واحد feedback إيجابي محدد بشكل SBI: الموقف بتاريخ أو رقم PR، واللي عمله من غير صفات، وإيه اللي اتغير بسببه. لو مفيش زميل تقوله، قوله لصاحبك على حاجة عملها، أو اشكر maintainer على PR اتعمله merge في مشروع open source بنفس الشكل.
Solution · الحل Worked example from the invented team, to Karim: "Yesterday when the deploy stalled, you posted the exact command in the channel before running it. Salma spotted it was pointing at staging and we saved a second rollback. Thank you." Notice there's no "amazing" or "always" in it, and that it took thirty seconds to write.
مثال من الفريق المتألف، لكريم: "إمبارح لما الـ deploy وقف، كتبت الـ command بالظبط في الـ channel قبل ما تشغّله. سلمى لاحظت إنه رايح على الـ staging ووفرنا rollback تاني. شكرًا." لاحظ إن مفيهاش "عظيم" ولا "دايمًا"، وإنها خدت 30 ثانية تتكتب.

Do it by hand, not with AI. Keep it private to the two of you, with no customer data and no company secrets. It only counts when three months have gone by and the log has a real before and after. If you have nobody to pair with at work, pair with a friend on the side project in project/.
في شغلك، الأسبوع ده: بموافقة الشخص، احجز ساعة pairing أسبوعية مع زميل جونيور أو mid، واعمل أول واحدة، وابدأ log مشترك وفيه أول سطر. اعملها بإيدك مش بـ AI. خلّيه بينكم، من غير بيانات عملاء ولا أسرار شركة. والتمرين ده بيتحسب بس لما 3 شهور يعدّوا ويبقى في الـ log قبل وبعد حقيقي. ولو مفيش حد تعمل معاه pairing في الشغل، اعمل pairing مع صاحبك على المشروع الجانبي في project/.
Hint · تلميح The invitation, from the invented team: "Karim, would you want a weekly hour where you drive and I navigate, on your real tickets? Tuesdays at 14:00, for three months, and we can stop any time. I'd keep a short shared log so we can both see what's changing, and I'd only ever mention it to anyone else if you say that's OK."
الدعوة من الفريق المتألف: "يا كريم، تحب نعمل ساعة كل أسبوع إنت اللي بتكتب فيها وأنا navigator، على الـ tickets بتاعتك الحقيقية؟ كل تلات الساعة 2، لمدة 3 شهور، ونقدر نوقف في أي وقت. هكتب log قصير مشترك عشان نشوف إيه اللي بيتغير، ومش هذكره لحد غير لو إنت موافق."
Solution · الحل First line of the log, from the invented team: "17 Mar. Pairing: notifications retries, Salma drove. Still fuzzy for her: worker shutdown on deploy. Next week: read that part together." Three months later, the last line has to be something she did alone.
أول سطر في الـ log من الفريق المتألف: "17 مارس. Pairing على retries الـ notifications، سلمى اللي كتبت. لسه مش واضح عندها: الـ worker shutdown وقت الـ deploy. الأسبوع الجاي: نقرا الجزء ده سوا." وبعد 3 شهور، آخر سطر لازم يكون حاجة عملتها هي لوحدها.

Cheat sheet · الخلاصة

  • Ladder: tried what → evidence → which part → hint → show and narrate → together. Stop as soon as they're moving.
  • Skip the ladder when production's on fire, it's a plain fact, or the person's overwhelmed. Then help and say how you found it.
  • Don't act surprised. Ask what they already know. Link to the exact part of the docs and check it helped.
  • Pairing: one fixed hour a week, the less experienced person types, a 3-line note at the end. Stay in the navigator's seat and take a real break.
  • Feedback: Situation, Behaviour, Impact, then Intent and one habit. Hard feedback in private and soon. Ask first if you're a peer.
  • AI rule: by hand first in a new topic or an unfamiliar bug, AI's fine for boilerplate they've written, always able to explain every line. Agreed together, with a review date.
  • The Anthropic trial (52 people, quiz right after, about 50% vs 67%) is one company's early study of its own product category and doesn't prove long-term harm.
  • Stretch: one notch harder, why them, safety net, check-ins booked in advance, cost told to the manager.
  • Strengths: name them, make them visible, ask before telling a manager, and keep stretch tasks on the gaps.
  • Evidence: a shared log with dates and a before and after. Consent first, and don't claim their work as yours.
بالعامية
  • السلم: جرّبت إيه ← الدليل ← أنهي جزء ← hint ← تعرض وتحكي وصلت إزاي ← سوا. وتقف أول ما الشخص يتحرك.
  • سيب السلم لما الـ production يكون واقع، أو تكون معلومة بسيطة، أو الشخص مضغوط. ساعتها ساعد وقول وصلت إزاي.
  • متتصنّعش الاندهاش. اسأل الشخص عارف إيه. ابعت الجزء المحدد من الـ docs واتأكد إنه ساعد.
  • الـ pairing: ساعة ثابتة في الأسبوع، الأقل خبرة بيكتب، وملاحظة 3 سطور في الآخر. خليك في مكان الـ navigator وخد بريك حقيقي.
  • الـ feedback: Situation وBehaviour وImpact، وبعدين Intent وعادة واحدة. الصعب على انفراد وبسرعة. واستأذن الأول لو إنت زميل.
  • قاعدة الـ AI: بالإيد الأول في موضوع جديد أو bug مش مفهوم، والـ AI تمام في الـ boilerplate اللي كتبوه قبل كده، وقادرين يشرحوا كل سطر دايمًا. متفق عليها سوا وليها ميعاد مراجعة.
  • الـ trial بتاع Anthropic (52 شخص، quiz بعد الـ task على طول، حوالي 50% قصاد 67%) دراسة مبكرة من شركة عن نوع منتجها، ومبتثبتش ضرر طويل المدى.
  • الـ stretch: درجة واحدة أصعب، ليه هو بالذات، safety net، check-ins محجوزة من الأول، والتكلفة متقالة للمدير.
  • نقط القوة: سمّيها، خليها تبان، استأذن قبل ما تكلم المدير، وخلّي الـ stretch tasks على الفجوات.
  • الدليل: log مشترك بتواريخ وقبل وبعد. الموافقة الأول، ومتنسبش شغلهم لنفسك.
